# CF9 — Compression, Promotion and Final Doctrine

Status: **COMPLETE**

## 1. Promotion is not a reward for research volume

CF1–CF8 produced a large research surface. CF9 asks which results deserve to enter the ordinary World cognitive path.

The promotion test is deliberately strict:

```text
repeated decision value
+ deletion causes a concrete wrong action / wrong causal claim
+ representation can be owner-neutral without stealing owner truth
+ survives negative-transfer boundaries
+ can be expressed substantially more compactly than its evidence history
→ doctrine candidate
```

A result can be true and important yet remain Research if it is too domain-specific, too assumption-heavy, or useful only when a particular causal question is active.

## 2. Promotion court

### Promote as compact doctrine laws

CF9 promotes these **semantic rules**, not objects:

1. **Capability is boundary-, transition- and condition-relative.** Existing R10 Capability remains the underlying primitive.
2. **Realized performance is not retained Actor capability identity.** Stronger claims inherit the conditions and evidence actually tested.
3. **Environment is a query-activated causal role, not a complete state object.** Use only decision-relevant owner-native facts.
4. **Institution is a query-activated causal role for durable rule/process relations.** Mechanism roles localize interventions without creating a universal Institution owner.
5. **Static owner-native projection is the default.** Activate dynamics only when history changes the decision.
6. **Mechanism decomposition is justified when it changes the next intervention, owner, recovery or attribution decision.** Predictive elegance alone is insufficient.
7. **Capability production and current expression are distinct causal claims.** `Unmasking` remains a derived expression case.
8. **Joint-system and retained component capability can move differently.** Their relative value is objective/recovery/consequence dependent.
9. **Exposure assignment is separate from exposure effect.** Search/admission/selection and entrant composition can themselves be intervention targets.
10. **Causal claims must match the query actually identified by design and assumptions.** Total effect, mechanism, dynamic policy and transport are separate burdens.
11. **Source evidence does not automatically become target truth.** Transport requires explicit source–target mechanism assumptions and negative-transfer boundaries.
12. **Feedback/co-evolution requires direction-specific Attribution.** Temporal recurrence or common loop shape is not enough.
13. **Descriptive and normative institution questions remain separate.** No universal InstitutionQuality/CapabilityValue scalar follows.
14. **UNKNOWN is a valid causal/capability state when the stronger claim is not identified.** Do not force intrinsic, environmental or institutional attribution.

These laws are compressed into [`capability-context-doctrine.md`](capability-context-doctrine.md).

## 3. Retain as conditional research methods, not default doctrine machinery

The following survive but remain conditional analytical methods:

- the seven CF3 institutional mechanism roles;
- CF4 production-gate details;
- CF5 assignment/evidence classes;
- CF7 transport postures `T0/T1/T2/T3/TX`;
- CF8 causal query classes and admission-state vocabulary;
- family-specific coevolution models.

Why not place all of them in the default doctrine surface?

Because their value is **question-activated**. For an ordinary current capability query, owner-native facts and the compact doctrine are enough. Loading full causal-method taxonomies by default would recreate the OFR7 failure mode: more theory context with no demonstrated decision gain.

## 5. Strong negative results preserved

CF9 keeps four negative results visible because future maintainers could otherwise re-create the same abstractions.

### M3 did not become a predictive layer

Transition-topology decomposition added **zero** static predictive cases over strong M2 in CF0. CF3 recovered only intervention/owner-localization value.

### Capability Field did not survive deletion pressure as an object

Every current static query can be expressed through existing owner-native claims plus `ResourceFor / ActionableResourceFor / Option / Capability` and contextual constraints.

### Institution did not become a second truth owner

The relevant mechanisms are real, but access, authority, cost, enforcement, ownership, coordination and recovery have different native owners and clocks.

### Coevolution did not become a universal flywheel

Only family-specific directionally attributed loops survive. Shared arrow shapes do not establish common mechanism or positive value.

## 6. Relation to R10 Resource doctrine

CF9 does **not** replace or rewrite [`resource-option-capability-model.md`](resource-option-capability-model.md).

> How do owner-native Reality facts become ResourceFor, Actionability, Option, Capability, Action, Effect, Outcome, Attribution and Knowledge without a global registry?

> When observed performance or opportunity differs across Actors/environments/institutions, what causal distinctions are required before assigning the difference to Actor capability, current context, prior exposure, institutional rules, selection or feedback?

The two doctrines share owner-native truth and projection principles but retain different research burdens.

## 8. Final programme answer

The original question asked whether environment/institutions can matter more than individual differences.

CF0–CF9 rejects any universal percentage or rank ordering. The correct answer is regime- and intervention-relative:

- under hard access/authority constraints, institutional relations can dominate current feasible Options;
- under missing tools/path/complements, environment can dominate current expression;
- after sustained exposure, environment may have already become part of later Actor state;
- when Actors self-select into environments, observed gaps cannot identify exposure effects without stronger design;
- when constraints are relaxed, previously hidden Actor heterogeneity may become more visible rather than disappear;
- over long horizons, Actor/resource/institution feedback may matter, but direction must be identified rather than assumed.

Thus the durable question is not:

```text
What percent is Person versus Environment versus Institution?
```

It is:

> **Which distinction currently changes the reachable transition set, later state, assignment process, causal intervention or feedback—and what evidence is strong enough to identify that claim?**
