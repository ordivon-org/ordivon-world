# Ordivon Link Charter

Status: active product interpretation of Computing `LINK-CHARTER-003`

## Mission

Ordivon Link is the Task-to-connectivity and evidence-continuity overlay above
mature network, identity, discovery, and messaging substrates. It translates an
open Task's communication need into an exact relation/path/identity binding,
preserves the conditions under which evidence was produced, and permits the
Task to continue after path, endpoint, identity, or participant replacement.

## Candidate responsibility

Link researches and may own:

- Connectivity Requirement and versioned Path/Identity Observations;
- immutable Connectivity Binding to exact logical source/target, path,
  endpoint, transport, identity, policy, and observation revisions;
- path-conditioned Artifact and Claim provenance;
- explicit invalidation after network or identity change;
- uncertain delivery/reply reconciliation;
- relationship continuity and accountable participant handoff.

Exact Task, Effect, Artifact, Claim, participant, and commitment semantics remain
foreign references. Classical network and identity mechanisms remain owned by
their native systems.

## Current evidence

The observation chain and private isolated egress tools are real operational
capabilities. The Baseline transport is a bounded reference experiment. The
Network World and Security port are deterministic network-condition and
evidence experiments.

None of these alone proves a permanent Agent-native Network World layer.

## Boundary

Link supplies how a Task-conditioned logical relationship is realized and what
path/identity conditions can be evidenced. Host decides why the relationship
exists and how Task work changes. Edge owns external execution bodies. Runtime
owns trusted-local execution. Security or the domain system owns consequence
policy and final validity.

## Success condition

One open Task can express a communication need, bind and verify mature network
and identity mechanisms, preserve path-conditioned evidence, invalidate only
affected claims, reconcile uncertain communication, and continue across path or
participant replacement without building a new network stack.
