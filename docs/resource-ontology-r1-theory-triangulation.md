# Resource Ontology R1 — Foundational Theory Triangulation

## 1. Research question

R0 established 48 materially different Ordivon episodes before choosing a definition of `Resource`.

R1 asks a different question:

> Which structural claims about resources, affordances, capabilities, conversion, optionality, information, bottlenecks, learning and resilience survive when foundational theories are forced to predict the same R0 cases — and where do those theories disagree?

R1 is **not** a literature review and does **not** choose an ontology.

A theory earns transfer only when it changes explanation or prediction for real R0 cases. A familiar term is not enough.

For every theory family R1 records:

1. its native unit of analysis;
2. whether value/actionability is intrinsic, relational or theory-relative;
3. how time, uncertainty, composition or scarcity enter;
4. at least three R0 cases it predicts or clarifies;
5. what must **not** transfer into World;
6. a falsifier or unresolved pressure to carry into R2.

# 3. T1 — Ecological affordances: action possibility is relational

## Native claim

Chemero's relational theory treats an affordance as a relation between an organism's abilities and features of its environment. It is not a property of the environment alone or the organism alone.

The useful transfer is not the word `affordance`; it is the structural move:

```text
feature alone
+ actor ability alone
       ↓
do not determine action possibility independently

actor ability × environmental feature
       ↓
relation-specific action possibility
```

## R0 pressure tests

- **N05** — the same Snowflake mechanism fails directly but works from an admitted JP parent relation. Nominal mechanism identity is insufficient; current relation changes actionability.
- **H02** — the same tool can be context, intervention or joint-system component depending on the question.
- **W06** — an HTTP-reachable endpoint may still lack the semantic relation required by the consumer workload.
- **S05** — installed Figma plus OAuth initiation does not produce design actionability when user-authority relation is incomplete.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** actionability is likely relation-sensitive. A candidate resource cannot be characterized adequately by object properties alone when the actor/system boundary, target transition and environment change what can actually be done.

**Do not carry:** ecological affordance theory does not supply Ordivon's authority, legal permission, cost, provenance, goal ownership, currentness or evidence semantics. An affordance can be physically possible while an Ordivon effect remains unauthorized.

## R2 falsifier

If a minimal Resource definition can omit actor/system-boundary relation while still distinguishing N05, H02, W06 and S05 without smuggling relation back through another term, the relational requirement should be weakened from `Resource` to a later `Actionable Resource/Option` stage.

# 4. T2 — Sen capability conversion: goods are not the advantage they enable

## Native claim

Sen's capability argument attacks a goods-only view of advantage. The same goods can convert into materially different capabilities for different persons, and the relevant object of evaluation may be what goods enable rather than the goods themselves.

This gives R1 a second, independent route to:

```text
resource stock
!= converted capability
```

It is stronger than a mere access distinction because conversion efficiency itself varies by actor/context.

## R0 pressure tests

- **H03** — Agent output can improve joint Human×Agent performance without becoming retained Human capability.
- **S01** — installed REAPER becomes a proven production resource only when a native project and reconstructable render demonstrate usable production capability.
- **G03** — live Agent cognition changes trajectories but does not thereby prove player-value capability.
- **F04** — thousands of information candidates do not convert into surviving alpha/capital capability.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** Resource and Capability should remain distinct; conversion conditions and the consumer's ability to transform a resource into a target state are first-class.

**Do not carry:** Sen's normative objective is human capability and equality. World cannot generalize this into a universal moral or optimization objective for software, networks, Finance or Security.

## R2 falsifier

Any proposed definition that identifies resource possession with capability must explain H03, G03 and F04 without creating a second hidden conversion concept.

# 5. T3 — Penrose: resources render services through accumulated experience

## Native claim

Penrose distinguishes a firm's resources from the productive services they can render. The services available from a resource depend partly on accumulated managerial experience and on what other resources/services are available. She also treats unused productive services and special knowledge as inducements for future expansion while shortages of specialized services can block it.

The transferable structure is:

```text
resource stock
+ experience / coordination / complementary services
        ↓
productive service actually renderable
```

## R0 pressure tests

- **S01 / S03** — owning/launching professional software does not guarantee the service the production needs; postconditions and usable workflows matter.
- **G06** — existing topology/pressure/placement components yield new problem surfaces through recomposition without new core resources.
- **H03** — Human×Agent system output depends on coordination, verification and use ability, not model possession alone.
- **S07** — an additional tool can add little marginal productive service when current resources already cover the need.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** distinguish resource stock from services/capabilities rendered; conversion knowledge/coordination is itself causally important; unused resources may contain future productive potential.

**Do not carry:** firm growth is not Ordivon's universal objective. “Unused service induces expansion” must not become a rule to consume every idle resource.

## R2 falsifier

If R2 can explain S01, G06 and S07 using only intrinsic resource properties plus demand without any conversion/composition relation, Penrose-style productive-service separation is unnecessary.

# 6. T4 — Resource-Based View: strategic value is selective, not cardinal

## Native claim

Barney's 1991 RBV asks which firm resources can underpin sustained competitive advantage and highlights value, rarity, imperfect imitability and substitutability under resource heterogeneity.

R1 uses RBV mostly as an **anti-cardinality** pressure, not as a universal resource ontology.

## R0 pressure tests

- **N01** — 415 transport variants do not imply 415 strategic capabilities.
- **G04** — fixture actors can remain meaningful substitutes for live cognition, so expensive differentiation alone has no value claim.
- **S07** — Inkscape is available but has weak marginal value because current substitutes cover the need.
- **N07** — more observation owners improve some diversity dimensions without creating new capability classes.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** resource value is selective; substitutability and marginal contribution matter; resource count is not a strategic-value measure.

**Do not carry:** rarity, imitability and competitor exclusion are not generally required for Ordivon value. Public EDGAR data, open-source tools and common protocols can be extremely valuable precisely because they are abundant and reproducible.

## R2 falsifier

If a Resource definition requires scarcity/rarity, **F01**, **Q01**, public OSS and common network protocols falsify it. RBV dimensions belong at most to local valuation, not Resource existence.

# 7. T5 — Dynamic capabilities: coordinating and reconfiguring is a separate capability

## Native claim

Teece, Pisano and Shuen place competitive performance not only in asset positions but in processes that coordinate/combine resources and in path-dependent evolution. Identifying opportunities and organizing to embrace them is central to the framework.

R1 translates the structure, not the competitive objective:

```text
asset/resource positions
      +
coordination / combination / reconfiguration processes
      +
path/history
      ↓
change-capable system
```

## R0 pressure tests

- **W08 / F05** — lower-owner capabilities can be exported as higher-owner resources and recomposed without leaking mechanism ownership.
- **N11** — a stable Finance egress resource can survive replacement of underlying physical members because Workstation owns reconfiguration.
- **N10** — failed WARP evidence changes later selection; conversion capability learns from history.
- **G06** — existing resources are reconfigured into new problem surfaces without changing the hard core.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** conversion/reconfiguration capability is distinct from resource stock; path history and learning can change future resource usefulness; capability can enlarge the reachable resource universe.

**Do not carry:** sustained private wealth creation is not World's objective. Path dependence does not authorize lock-in or persistence of obsolete mechanisms.

## R2 falsifier

If changing reconfiguration/coordination capacity while holding the same resources fixed cannot change any R0 capability prediction, this theory adds no needed distinction. N11 and G06 currently make that unlikely.

# 8. T6 — Real options: deliberate non-use can preserve value

## Native claim

McDonald and Siegel show that under uncertainty and irreversibility, “invest when benefits exceed costs” can miss the value of waiting. The option to act later can itself be valuable.

This gives R1 a precise reason to reject utilization as a universal objective:

```text
use now
may consume
future choice
```

## R0 pressure tests

- **S06** — TouchDesigner can remain a high-information candidate before account/license acquisition.
- **N08** — bulk public datasets remain indexed when bounded queries already satisfy the current claim.
- **Q05** — libvirt is deliberately deferred because current adoption cost/state ownership exceeds demonstrated deletion value.
- **S02** — OBS capability is retained while its listener stays off outside bounded need.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** optionality can be valuable before consumption; irreversibility, uncertainty, reversibility and future choice must affect acquisition/use thresholds.

**Do not carry:** not every Ordivon resource is an irreversible investment with a monetary payoff process. Real-option mathematics should not become a universal scalar score.

## R2 falsifier

A definition that requires immediate current use to qualify as Resource must explain S06/N08 without losing economically meaningful future choice. Conversely, if a dormant item has no credible future transition and no reconsideration trigger, calling it an option should fail.

# 9. T7 — Requisite variety: redundancy is disturbance-relative response variety

## Native claim

Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety is a regulation result: reducing disturbance/outcome variety requires sufficient regulator variety/information. The law concerns **relevant response variety**, not raw inventory count.

## R0 pressure tests

- **N02** — two logical profiles can collapse into one physical failure domain; their raw count does not supply response variety against physical-WAN failure.
- **N06** — roots diverse by protocol/node can still share provider/access domain; different disturbance classes see different effective variety.
- **N07** — 24 observation resources improve owner/mechanism/method diversity while capability-slot coverage remains unchanged.
- **G05** — two Scenario Cases do not necessarily create two substantially different problem classes.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** redundancy/diversity must be evaluated against the disturbance or target transition it can answer; cardinality is not enough.

**Do not carry:** the law is about regulation, not a mandate to maximize variety everywhere. Variety also has maintenance, attack-surface and coordination costs that Ashby's minimal relation does not price.

## R2 falsifier

If a proposed redundancy concept cannot distinguish N02's logical alternatives from physical-failure alternatives, it is too weak. If it requires all differences to be independent across every disturbance, it is too strong.

# 10. T8 — Exploration/exploitation: current utilization competes with future option creation

## Native claim

March models allocation between exploring new possibilities and exploiting known ones, with costs and benefits distributed across time. Adaptive systems can over-refine exploitation because its feedback is faster, producing short-run effectiveness and long-run self-destruction.

## R0 pressure tests

- **N10** — a failed WARP experiment is valuable because it improves later discovery rather than producing a current effect.
- **H04** — Human attention cannot internalize all Agent output; learning allocation must preserve future control while avoiding overload.
- **F04** — large information universes must be narrowed through falsification rather than exploited indiscriminately as “more data”.
- **N08** — exhaustive bulk acquisition can consume budget without adding current capability and can crowd out higher-information search.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** resource allocation has at least two time-horizon roles: producing current effects and improving future search/knowledge/options. Fast feedback from current utilization can underinvest in exploration.

**Do not carry:** R1 imports no fixed exploration percentage, no universal explore/exploit scalar, and no assumption that novelty is valuable by itself.

## R2 falsifier

If all learning/search activities can be represented without distinguishing their future-option effects from current workload effects, a separate exploration resource role may be unnecessary. R0 currently suggests the distinction matters at allocation, not at Resource identity.

# 11. T9 — Absorptive capacity + knowledge integration: information value depends on conversion capacity

## Native claims

Cohen & Levinthal define absorptive capacity around recognizing the value of external information, assimilating it and applying it; prior related knowledge and history matter. Grant emphasizes the integration and application of specialist knowledge through coordination mechanisms.

Together they add a critical correction to “information is a resource”:

```text
external information exists
        ↓
recognition
        ↓
assimilation / interpretation
        ↓
integration with other knowledge
        ↓
application
```

The same evidence can therefore yield different capability for different actors/systems.

## R0 pressure tests

- **F03** — reachable SEC bytes need immutable source identity and research interpretation before becoming decision evidence.
- **F04** — thousands of sources do not produce alpha without hypothesis/falsification capability.
- **Q02** — provider classifications enter Security as scoped claims, not automatically as independent Security truth.
- **H04** — Human cannot internalize all generated evidence; representation and promotion depend on durable future control value.
- **N10** — failed experiments become useful only when retained in validation memory and applied to later ranking.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** information-resource value depends on recognition, assimilation, integration and application capability; knowledge accumulation is path-dependent; evidence abundance can exceed absorptive capacity.

**Do not carry:** “commercial ends”, firm membership and individual-resident knowledge are not universal World assumptions. Owner-native truth and distributed Agent/tool memory remain separate.

## R2 falsifier

If two actors with identical access to F03/Q02 information but different interpretation/integration ability must still be assigned identical current informational Resource value, the model is missing a conversion relation.

# 12. T10 — Shannon versus Howard: information amount is not decision value

## Native claims

Shannon's communication theory measures information through the probabilistic structure of communication and channel constraints. Howard's information-value theory explicitly adds consequences/preferences: reducing uncertainty has value only relative to the decision in which that uncertainty matters.

This yields an important internal contradiction within “information theory” itself:

```text
amount / entropy / channel information
!=
decision value of information
```

## R0 pressure tests

- **N07** — more public observations can increase evidence diversity without increasing effect capability.
- **F04** — a huge information universe can rationally terminate at zero capital action after falsification.
- **H04** — more Agent output can consume scarce Human attention without improving future control.
- **Q02** — a provider claim may carry information yet have low authority for an independent Security conclusion.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** informational Resources must not be valued by bytes/source count/entropy alone; claim-relative decision impact and uncertainty reduction matter.

**Do not carry:** Howard's monetary expected-value formulation is not a universal World scalar. Some decisions have non-monetary, safety, authority, ethical or option-value consequences; some information matters for learning without changing the immediate action.

## R2 falsifier

Any Resource model that equates information quantity with informational value must survive F04 and H04. A decision-relative model must also explain evidence retained for future unknown decisions without forcing a current scalar value.

# 13. T11 — Operations/bottlenecks: marginal resource value depends on the binding constraint

## Native claim

Ford–Fulkerson's network-flow formulation assigns capacities to links and asks for maximum source-to-destination flow. Its transferable lesson is narrower than “everything is a flow network”:

> adding capacity away from the binding cut/bottleneck can have zero marginal effect on achievable throughput.

## R0 pressure tests

- **N04** — local Snowflake client capability is not enough when shared volunteer capacity is unavailable.
- **N11** — several physical members matter only insofar as the stable consumer egress has eligible current capacity; extra members outside the binding failure/target constraint may add little.
- **F04** — thousands of data resources have no capital effect when the binding bottleneck is surviving hypothesis quality/falsification.
- **H04** — adding more generated knowledge does not increase Human learning if attention/promotion is the binding conversion capacity.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** marginal resource value is bottleneck-sensitive; Capacity and Capability must remain distinct; adding non-binding resources can yield zero capability gain.

**Do not carry:** Ordivon resource ecologies are not generally steady-state capacitated flow networks. Knowledge, software, options and authority do not obey conservation like rail flow.

## R2 falsifier

If a model values each additional resource independently of complement dependencies/binding constraints, N04/F04/H04 falsify it. If the model forces every relationship into a numeric capacity edge, it is overfitted.

# 14. T12 — Resilience: persistence under disturbance is not the same objective as local efficiency/stability

## Native claim

Holling distinguishes ecological resilience/persistence under disturbance from a narrower equilibrium-stability notion. R1 imports only the distinction between performing well around one operating point and retaining relationships/functions under disturbances.

## R0 pressure tests

- **N02 / N06** — logical route diversity can perform normally while failing to provide resilience against a shared physical/provider disturbance.
- **N09** — historical child facts remain true while current usability disappears when the parent relation changes.
- **N11** — stable consumer semantics over replaceable member leases illustrate function persistence despite mechanism replacement.
- **S02** — a useful capability can remain dormant/default-off while preserving recoverable future function and reducing exposure.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** reliability at the current operating point is different from maintaining/recovering capability under disturbance; slack, substitution and replaceable mechanism can have resilience value.

**Do not carry:** persistence is not an unconditional objective. Deliberately retiring WARP, libvirt or a low-value tool can improve the system. A harmful or obsolete relation should not be made resilient merely because it exists.

## R2 falsifier

If Resource/Capability quality is modeled only by present success probability, N02/N11 should expose missing disturbance semantics. If resilience is treated as universal persistence, N10/Q05/S07 falsify it.

# 15. T13 — Formal resource theory: convertibility and composition may be more fundamental than a scalar value

## Native claim

Coecke, Fritz and Spekkens formulate resource theories around resources, transformations, parallel composition, and a distinguished set of transformations considered freely implementable. Their theory of resource convertibility uses a preorder: whether one resource can be converted into another. They explicitly argue that this preorder can be more fundamental than any single scalar resource measure because resources may be incomparable and different monotones can rank them differently.

This is the strongest R1 challenger to an intuitive object/utility definition.

```text
not primarily:
  "how much Resource value does A have?"

but:
  "under the allowed transformations, what can A become?"
  "what can A compose with?"
  "what conversions need a catalyst?"
```

## R0 pressure tests

- **G06** — existing content resources compose into new mechanically distinct problem surfaces.
- **W03** — a parent entitlement can act like a prerequisite/catalytic condition for downstream acquisitions.
- **W08** — a lower-owner capability can be treated as a higher-owner resource transformation surface.
- **S07 / Q05** — two resources can be incomparable or contextually dominated without requiring one global scalar ranking.
- **F04** — many information resources are convertible into observations/hypotheses but not into validated capital action under the current conversion rules.

## Transfer contract

**Carry forward:** conversion relations and composition can be more fundamental than scalar resource value; resource families can be partially ordered/incomparable; transformations can themselves be higher-level resources; catalysis/complementarity deserve explicit tests.

**Do not carry:** the paper's `free transformations` are modeling primitives, not Ordivon authority. World cannot declare an operation “free” and thereby create provider/legal/owner authority. The formalism also abstracts away currentness, provenance, uncertainty, risk and domain Outcome unless a concrete theory adds them.

## R2 falsifier

A convertibility-only definition must distinguish **Q03** (sample bytes are convertible mechanically but execution is unauthorized), **W04** (provider offer exists but acquisition relation rejects the actor), and **G03** (live cognition converts state into different actions but additional player value is unproven). If it cannot, convertibility belongs inside the model but is insufficient as the whole Resource ontology.

| C12 | Persistence/resilience as virtue vs rational retirement/deletion | N10, Q05, S07 | resilience is goal- and relation-scoped; persistence of a low-value liability is not capability |
| C13 | Single scalar resource measure vs partial/incomparable convertibility order | S07, Q05, N07 | global scalar value is not justified; decision-local rankings may coexist without a total order |

## The most important unresolved contradiction

**C2 is intentionally left open.**

R0 pushed us toward:

```text
Resource = actor × goal × context relation
```

but R1 found a serious challenger:

```text
Resource can be defined in a theory-relative conversion space
before one concrete workload is selected;
workload/goal may instead determine which conversions become Options.
```

This matters because a GPU, a public dataset, a dormant tool or an account entitlement can plausibly remain a resource even while no current workload is demanding it.

R2 must therefore test at least one definition where **Resource is broader than demand-scoped Option**. R1 does not allow the relational intuition to win by default.

# 18. Theory-derived propositions carried into R2

These are **candidate propositions**, not World laws.

## P1 — Resource stock and capability must remain distinguishable

Supported independently by Sen, Penrose, dynamic capabilities and R0 S01/H03/G03/F04.

Deletion test: if the distinction is removed, possession/availability repeatedly becomes confused with reliable target-state transition.

## P2 — Actionability is relational even if Resource existence may not be

Chemero + Sen + R0 N05/H02/S05 strongly support relation-sensitive actionability.

R1 does **not** yet prove that the minimal noun `Resource` itself must be demand-scoped. C2 remains for R2.

## P3 — Conversion/composition structure is more fundamental than resource count

Penrose, Teece, Grant, Ford/Fulkerson and formal resource theory converge on this from different directions.

R0 G06/W03/N04/F04 supply cross-domain pressure.

## P4 — Resource relations are only partially orderable; one global scalar is unlikely

Formal resource theory makes the mathematical point explicit; Sen also exposes indexing difficulty; R0 S07/Q05/N07 shows context-dependent/incomparable marginal value.

Local decision scores can still be useful, but they do not become ontology.

## P5 — Optionality is a real stock-like property but not necessarily a Resource object

Real options plus R0 S06/N08/Q05 show credible future choice can be valuable before use.

R2/R5 must decide whether `Option Capital` is derived from resource relations rather than a separate inventory entity.

## P6 — Redundancy is substitutable, current, disturbance-relevant optionality

Ashby + resilience + R0 N02/N06 support this strongly.

Independence is relative to a failure/disturbance class; “independent in every dimension” is neither required nor usually knowable.

## P7 — Information-resource value depends on a conversion/decision context

Shannon/Howard, absorptive capacity and Grant converge with F03/F04/Q02/H04.

Bytes, source count and even uncertainty reduction are not sufficient for domain value.

## P8 — Exploration/learning can rationally consume resources without immediate Effect

March + absorptive capacity + N10/H04 imply that some resource use is aimed at creating future Options/Knowledge rather than current external Effects.

This prevents the Effect chain from treating research nulls as failed utilization.

## P9 — Bottlenecks determine marginal value

Ford/Fulkerson gives a precise structural analogy; N04/F04/H04 prove the broader pattern.

A Resource can be abundant and still have near-zero marginal value if another prerequisite/capacity is binding.

## P10 — Resilience and current efficiency/capacity are separate dimensions

Holling/Ashby plus N02/N11/S02 support this.

A capability can be efficient but fragile, or dormant/slack but valuable for recovery.

## P11 — Authority/currentness/provenance are Ordivon-native axes not supplied by the imported theories

This is a major negative result.

None of the imported foundational theories adequately models all of:

```text
owner-native authority
legal/provider permission
credential possession
provenance/truth role
point-in-time currentness
external effect admission
```

R0 W02/W04/Q03/G01 forces World to retain these as independent reality relations rather than borrowing an external theory wholesale.

## P12 — Conversion/Effect is below Outcome

Formal convertibility, operations flow and tool execution can establish that a transformation occurred. They do not establish that the consuming domain achieved its value function.

R0 G03/S04/F04/H03 makes this one of R1's strongest findings.

R6 should therefore test:

```text
Capability
→ Action
→ Effect
→ Outcome
→ Attribution
→ Knowledge
```

without promoting it yet.

# 19. What R1 now rejects

R1 adds these rejections to the R0 list:

1. **Do not define Resource by rarity or ownership.** RBV is a valuation lens for a competitive context, not ontology.
2. **Do not define Resource only by immediate usefulness.** Real-option cases preserve credible future value.
3. **Do not define Resource only by physical affordance.** Permission, authority and provenance can block Ordivon action despite physical feasibility.
4. **Do not define Resource only by convertibility.** Formal convertibility does not capture Q03 authorization or G03 domain Outcome.
5. **Do not use Shannon information quantity as informational Resource value.** Decision/learning relevance and absorptive capacity matter.
6. **Do not treat maximum utilization as a resource objective.** Waiting, slack, exploration, default-off surfaces and retirement can all be rational.
7. **Do not infer redundancy from diversity labels.** Disturbance-relative substitutability/currentness/independence are required.
8. **Do not turn bottleneck theory into a universal graph.** Bottleneck sensitivity survives; numeric edge-capacity ontology does not.
9. **Do not turn resilience into persistence-at-all-costs.** Some relations should die.
10. **Do not import one management/economic theory as World doctrine.** The strongest R1 result is cross-theory intersection plus explicit non-transfer.

# 20. R2 definition-design constraints earned by R1

R2 must construct **competing minimal definitions**, not one polished definition.

At minimum, the suite must contain candidates spanning these disagreements:

### Family A — Reality-object / broad-potential definition

Resource is an aspect of Reality with credible potential to participate in future state transitions, while actor/goal/context enters later at Actionable Resource/Option.

Pressure source: formal resource theory + dormant/general-purpose R0 cases.

### Family B — Relational affordance definition

Resource exists only as a relation among Reality feature, actor/system ability/boundary and a class of possible transitions.

Pressure source: Chemero + Sen + N05/H02.

### Family C — Conversion-theoretic definition

Resource is anything nontrivial in a specified transformation regime whose presence changes the reachable conversion preorder/composition space.

Pressure source: Coecke/Fritz/Spekkens + G06/W03.

### Family D — Option-set definition

Resource is whatever credibly enlarges the actor's reachable future option set after authority/cost/risk constraints.

Pressure source: real options + dynamic capabilities + current World model.

R2 may add a fifth family, but it must not collapse A–D before falsification.

Every definition must be attacked by at least:

```text
unknown dataset
idle GPU
415 correlated VPN variants
human attention
reputation / relationship
technical debt / liability
malware sample without execution authority
model capability
legal permission
unexercised option
failed experiment retained as knowledge
lower-owner exported capability
```

and by all 48 R0 cases where applicable.

# 21. R1 conclusion

R1 changes the research direction in one important way.

R0's strongest intuition was:

```text
Resource is relational.
```

R1 narrows that to a more defensible statement:

> **Actionability and realized value are strongly relational; Resource existence itself may be broader and may be representable through potential/convertibility before a concrete workload is selected.**

That distinction is now the central R2 fight.

Theories otherwise converge on several robust structures:

```text
resource stock != capability
count != value
information amount != decision value
current success != resilience
capacity != capability
conversion matters
composition matters
bottlenecks matter
history/learning matters
optional non-use can matter
failure can create knowledge
single global scalar is suspect
Effect != Outcome
```

But the most important World-native axes — owner authority, currentness, provenance/truth role and exact effect admission — are under-modeled by the imported theories. Those must remain anchored in Ordivon's existing owner-native laws.

R1 therefore authorizes **R2 definition falsification only**. It authorizes no Resource registry, no lifecycle state machine, no global dependency graph, no scalar value function and no production API/schema change.

The ten gap cases sharpen three conclusions:

### G-A — Authority is not merely another conversion cost

`W01`, `N12`, `G02`, `Q06` show that provenance, permission, possessed authority and capability location have semantic consequences that cannot be safely reduced to one numeric cost or one formal “allowed operation” convention.

### G-B — Currentness has more than one clock and more than one truth role

`W05` and `W07` show that acquisition access, consumption transport, historical admission and current presence can all differ without contradiction.

`Q04` and `W07` show that bytes/materialization can disappear while historical evidence or future re-acquisition/reference value remains; conversely a materialized object can remain unauthorized (`Q03`).

These are not arguments for new global fields. They are constraints R2 definitions must survive.

## 23.2 Final R1 coverage judgment

R1 now covers the complete frozen census while preserving an important asymmetry:

> External foundational theories explain many conversion, relation, optionality, information, bottleneck and resilience structures; **Ordivon's strongest unique burden remains exact owner authority, provenance, currentness, admission and truth-role boundaries.**

That is a reason to keep the World model thin and owner-preserving, not a reason to create a universal Resource authority service.
