# Boundary and Non-goals

A component belongs in Ordivon World only when its primary question is:

> How does an open Task bind, execute, observe, reconcile, and rebind one
> external interaction across changing target, path, identity, provider, or
> participant conditions?

It does not belong here when its primary question is:

- how packets, tunnels, routes, DNS, proxies, or meshes work;
- how browsers, Sandboxes, containers, VMs, queues, storage, or devices work;
- how a Task chooses strategy or decides completion;
- how trusted-local processes and Workspaces run;
- whether a domain action is authorized or semantically correct.

## Explicit non-goals

- no general proxy or traffic relay;
- no new VPN, CNI, Service Mesh, transport, TLS, PKI, or DNS implementation;
- no general Browser, Sandbox, VM, container, scheduler, or workflow engine;
- no automatic route/provider selection before evidence and invalidation are
  proved;
- no centralization of all external data through Ordivon;
- no universal World ID or premature Protocol schema;
- no permanent Agent-body or Network World claim from inherited prototypes.
