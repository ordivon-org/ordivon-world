# CF4 — Environmental Capitalization: Production vs Expression

Status: **COMPLETE**

## 1. The first genuinely dynamic distinction

CF0 found nine frozen cases where a static model stops being enough. CF4 asks why.

The minimal reason is:

```text
current Environment / Institution / support
can change current expression
and may also change later Actor/system state
```

Those are different causal claims.

## 2. Expression

An **expression/exercisability** change occurs when current performance or feasible transitions change because current conditions changed, without evidence that the retained Actor state changed.

Examples:

- path outage removes current actionability;
- fatigue lowers current performance and rest restores it;
- authority is revoked while underlying skill/artifact remains;
- an ordinary tool becomes unavailable;
- a previously hidden skill becomes usable because a complement appears.

This is enough to preserve the intuitive “unmasking” concept without creating a new primitive.

## 3. Production / capitalization

A **capability-production** claim says prior exposure, practice, feedback, investment, tool use or institutional experience changed a later Actor/system capability state.

Conceptually:

```text
State_{t+1}
= L(State_t, exposure history, practice, feedback, investment, ...)
```

CF4 retains the causal role but rejects a universal `L`.

The correct owner and state representation remain domain-specific: Human skill, Harness reusable cognition, organizational procedure, economic bargaining capability and model weights do not share one update law.

## 4. Mixed AI evidence is exactly what the theory predicts

Structured AI tutoring and direct answer assistance should not be collapsed into one treatment.

A 2025 randomized crossover study of a deliberately scaffolded AI physics tutor reported larger measured learning gains in less median time than the active-learning comparison under that bounded design.

A separate 2026 preprint reports randomized brief interactions where direct AI assistance improved immediate performance but worsened subsequent unassisted performance and persistence on tested math/reading tasks.

These are not contradictions at the CF level. They pressure the interaction:

```text
interaction policy
× learner state
× task
× immediate objective
× retained-learning objective
× horizon
```

The reusable law is therefore **policy dependence**, not `AI good` or `AI deskills`.

## 5. Four outcome patterns

CF4 retains a compact dynamic vocabulary.

### Complementation

```text
joint capability ↑
retained/recovery capability ↑
```

### Substitution / dependence

```text
joint capability ↑
independent capability ↓ or does not grow
```

This can be rational or fragile depending on recovery, verification, consequence and future support availability.

### Expression / unmasking

```text
current supported performance ↑
retained-state evidence roughly unchanged
```

### Capitalization / production

```text
prior exposure
→ later capability under a decision-relevant comparison changes
```

## 6. Production gate

CF4 will not call a performance delta “capability production” unless the evidence answers the stronger claim. At minimum:

- later outcome must actually measure the capability state being claimed;
- support/conditions relevant to the claim must be varied or bounded;
- horizon must be explicit;
- selection/confounding must be addressed enough for the causal claim;
- transport beyond the tested task/population must remain explicit.

Otherwise the answer is expression, candidate production, or `UNKNOWN`.

## 7. External duration pressure

The Moving to Opportunity evidence supplies a clean reason history cannot be dropped: randomized moves for younger children produced different long-run outcomes from moves later in childhood, consistent with exposure duration/timing mattering for the tested outcomes.

Cunha–Heckman skill-formation theory supplies the complementary structural idea that prior skill can change the productivity of later investment.

CF4 imports neither as a universal Human/Agent update equation.

## 8. Result

`Environmental Capitalization` survives CF4, but only as:

> **an evidence-backed causal relation from prior exposure/history to later capability state under a named boundary, transition, outcome and horizon.**

It is not Capital stock, Environment property or global World state.
