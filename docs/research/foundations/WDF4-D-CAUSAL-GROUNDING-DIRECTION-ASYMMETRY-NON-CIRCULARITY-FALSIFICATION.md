# 2. G1 — Intervention / invariance

Woodward-style interventionism is the strongest current candidate for MIP.

Its characteristic idea is:

```text
X causally influences Y
when suitable interventions on X
produce systematic/invariant changes in Y.
```

This cleanly separates:

```text
correlation
from
intervention-relevant influence.
```

## 2.1 Strength

Intervention/invariance handles:

```text
common-cause discrimination
type causal effect
mediation
control-relevant influence
prevention/maintenance
robustness
transport under declared invariance conditions
```

It fits MIP exceptionally well.

## 2.2 The circularity fact is explicit, not hidden

Woodward's own intervention concept is causal: an intervention variable/process must affect X in a way that isolates the X→Y pathway from other causal routes/confounders.

Thus interventionism is intentionally **non-reductive**.

The question is not whether the word `cause` appears covertly; it does.

The question is whether the circularity is disciplined enough to be non-vicious.

### D-G1-1

```text
Interventionism can characterize/discipline MIP
without reducing causal vocabulary to noncausal vocabulary.
```

### D-G1-2

```text
InterventionValidity
presupposes some causal-structural commitments.
```

Therefore interventionism does not answer:

```text
Why is the supplied causal isolation/orientation itself causal?
```

## 2.3 Direction

Interventions generate an operational asymmetry:

```text
do(X) changes Y
```

without corresponding ordinary admissible intervention symmetry.

But if `do` semantics are already defined relative to causal structure, this direction is not a noncausal ground of that structure.

### G1 disposition

```text
MIP discipline                  STRONG
ARP grounding                   PARTIAL/INDIRECT
Direction discipline            STRONG INSIDE SUPPLIED CAUSAL FRAME
Noncausal reduction             FAILS / NOT CLAIMED
Cross-regime usefulness         HIGH
Primitive commitment location   intervention/causal-isolation semantics
```

Interventionism survives as a major causal framework but cannot close D alone.

# 3. G2 — Process / transmission / conserved quantity

Dowe-style process theories provide one of the strongest candidates for physically grounding ARP.

A conserved-quantity account distinguishes causal from pseudo-processes by reference to physically conserved quantities and interactions.

## 3.1 Crucial decomposition already inside the theory

Dowe explicitly separates:

```text
Q1. What are causal processes and interactions?
Q2. What connects events we call cause and effect?
Q3. What distinguishes the cause from the effect / fixes direction?
```

This is directly aligned with WDF4's decomposition pressure.

### D-G2-1

```text
GroundingCausalProcess
!= GroundingCauseEffectConnection
!= GroundingDirection
```

## 3.2 Naive connection fails

A naive rule:

```text
C causes E iff a continuous causal-process line connects them
```

faces `misconnection` cases where physical processes connect events without licensing the intended singular cause-effect verdict.

Thus process existence is not sufficient for ACV.

## 3.3 Omission/prevention

Negative relata create additional pressure because an omission is not itself a physical process carrying conserved quantity.

Process theories can distinguish literal physical production from broader causal/influence talk, but then they do not ground the whole MIP family.

## 3.4 Direction

Dowe treats direction separately rather than deriving it automatically from conserved-quantity possession/exchange.

Therefore:

```text
PhysicalProcess
!= CausalOrientation by itself.
```

### G2 disposition

```text
Physical ARP grounding          STRONG
MIP grounding                   WEAK/PARTIAL
Cause-effect selection          INCOMPLETE without supplementation
Direction                       SEPARATE PROBLEM
Omission/prevention             PRESSURED
Cross-regime universality       NOT EARNED
```

Process grounding is retained as domain-local/physical grounding, not universal World grounding.

## 4.2 Reductive circularity

Canonical mechanist formulations call activities:


## 4.4 Cross-regime issue

```text

```

This maps naturally onto:

```text

```

## 5.1 Strength

```text

masking/antidotes
non-necessitating tendencies
probabilistic production
enabling

actual manifestation
```

## 5.2 Irreducibility is often the point

Some powers accounts explicitly treat dispositional modality as sui generis/irreducible rather than reducing it to possible worlds or categorical bases.

Therefore they do not remove primitive commitment.

They move it to:

```text
power / disposition / tendency
```

### D-G4-1

```text
PowerGrounding

without being reductive to nonmodal/noncausal facts.
```

Candidate answers often appeal to:


If powers then define causation, circularity threatens unless power identity has an independent basis.

## 7.1 Directed syntax is not enough

The equation:


The arrow does not ground itself.

### D-G6-1

```text

!= RealityCausalDirection by syntax alone.
```

Different causal orientations can be observationally indistinguishable/Markov-equivalent under suitable assumptions.

Orientation then requires extra information such as:

```text
intervention
```

These are grounding inputs, not products of bare graph topology.

## 7.3 Actual-cause model stability

But:

### D-MODEL-INV-2


```text
ReliableCorrelationOfDirections
```


### D-G8-1

## 9.4 Competing physical proposals remain open

D does not adjudicate fundamental physics here.

# 53. Final compressed judgment

WDF4-D finds that the causal-grounding problem cannot currently be solved by promoting one familiar causal technology into universal metaphysics.

Intervention/invariance remains the strongest generic framework for Modal Influence Projection, but it is explicitly nonreductive: suitable interventions are themselves causally characterized. Process/conserved-quantity theories provide unusually concrete physical grounding for actual causal processes, yet even Dowe separates the problems of causal process, cause-effect connection and direction, and naive process connection fails under misconnections. Mechanistic theories richly decompose production but normally use `productive`/`causal activity` at their own foundation. Powers/dispositions can unify tendency and manifestation, but often do so by making dispositional modality irreducible and face individuation/cross-regime overload. Laws can constrain physical possibilities without constituting forward production; global and time-neutral law frameworks show that lawhood alone does not orient causation. Structural equations encode direction but require causal interpretation and model validity. Temporal, thermodynamic and agency asymmetries are powerful witnesses in important regimes but do not survive as universal definitions of causal direction.

The central result is therefore not `causation is primitive`. It is more disciplined:

```text
At the current evidence frontier,
all serious causal theories have a Primitive Commitment Boundary.
```

The boundary may be intervention semantics, causal process, productivity, dispositional tendency, causal-law status, structural orientation or an explicit primitive causal relation. Failure to reduce further is not itself a defect; hiding the commitment is.

D accordingly upgrades WDF4 from architecture-only TCSA-alpha to grounding-aware TCSA-beta. MIP and ARP remain the two non-equivalent causal projections exposed by B/C, while actual contribution remains a derived verdict. But every foundation-level causal claim now needs Causal Grounding Disclosure, Orientation Provenance and a declared Primitive Commitment Boundary. This allows World to represent both reductive and nonreductive causal theories without pretending they have solved more metaphysics than they actually have.

Causal direction also remains irreducible at the present architecture level. Time direction, entropy direction, graph orientation, intervention/control asymmetry and causal direction often align, but no identity among them is foundation-safe. Global/time-neutral law candidates and backward-causation research make a syntax rule `cause = earlier` particularly untenable.

Thus WDF4-D does not close causation. It sharpens the remaining task from `find a universal ground` into a more tractable problem:

```text
How can heterogeneous grounding commitments be represented,
compared and composed without hidden primitives,
vicious circularity or cross-regime fragmentation?
```

That positive reconstruction is exactly WDF4-E.
