WDF0-A through E deliberately expanded the search space. WDF0-F performs the opposite operation:


WDF0-F is not allowed to preserve a concept merely because it was useful in prior rounds. A candidate survives only if deleting it forces a destructive collapse that cannot be reconstructed from a smaller grammar.

## 2. Compression Rule

A candidate X is removed from the WDF0 root grammar when all of the following hold:

1. X can be reconstructed from a smaller set of distinctions.
2. the reconstruction preserves the important cross-domain anti-laws;
3. the reconstruction does not smuggle X back under another name;
4. no current falsifier requires X as a separate truth role;
5. X can still remain a useful domain/local research term.

`Removed from root grammar` does not mean `false` or `useless`.


## 13. Object / Entity / Process

### Process/Event

The minimum kernel therefore does **not** need a universal taxonomy `Entity -> Object | Process`.


BFO remains a rigorous and useful upper-ontology rival whose continuant/occurrent distinction supports interoperability and scientific categorization, but WDF0 does not infer that this exact taxonomic split is Reality's universal root architecture.

### Disposition

`Object/Entity/Process` remain possible domain/ontology categories, not mandatory WDF0 roots.

## 15. What cannot currently be compressed away

After deleting the above notions, five positive question families remain necessary, plus one meta-discipline.

### G0 — Reality / Actuality

There must be a model-transcendent target to which observations, claims and actions are answerable.

WDF0 does not define what all actual Reality is made of.

### G1 — Structure / Relation

Reality supports distinctions and relations among actual conditions, realizers or patterns.

This umbrella must remain typed; causal, constitutive, spatial, semantic and epistemic relations are not interchangeable.

### G2 — Transformation / History

Reality changes; trajectories, occurrence, lineage and path dependence cannot be reconstructed from timeless structure alone.

### G3 — Modality / Constraint / Disposition — **required but unresolved**

Claims about what can happen, cannot happen, tends to happen, is reachable, feasible or authorized require modal structure.

Current WDF0 does not yet know how physical laws, boundary conditions, resource constraints, dispositions, institutional rules and Agent capabilities should relate in one foundation theory.

### G4 — Causality / Mechanism / Intervention — **required but unresolved**

Difference-making, production, intervention and mechanism cannot currently be reduced to mere temporal succession, correlation, generic relation or constitution.

Structural causal models provide a powerful formalism for intervention/counterfactual reasoning, but they presuppose model variables and structural equations; they do not settle the World-level ontology of causal structure.

### G5 — Constitution / Realization

WDF0 requires a non-causal relation family explaining what organized lower-level realities/roles constitute or realize:

- whole from parts/relations;
- macro role from substrate;
- legal status from rule-governed facts;
- representation/content from carrier/interpretation relations.

This firewall is needed to avoid causal double-counting.

### G6 — Epistemic / Representation

Observation, evidence, representation, belief and model cannot be eliminated because an Agent/observer must distinguish Reality from access/claims about Reality.

These are not required constituents of all Reality, but they are required in a World-foundation grammar that must explain knowledge and Agent coupling.

### M0 — Typed Dependence / Scope Discipline

Dependence Signature is **not** declared a thing in Reality.

It is the meta-rule that claims must expose legitimate dependencies such as:

```text
time/history
frame
scale/grain
boundary/system
actor/outcome
institution/rules
observer/path
measurement procedure
model/question
```

This prevents relative facts from being mistaken for either absolutes or subjective opinions.

## 16. WDF0 Minimal Meta-Grammar

The destructive-compression result is therefore not an ontology tree but a small question grammar:

```text
REALITY / ACTUALITY
│
├─ What structure/relations obtain?
│
├─ What changes, persists or has this history?
│
├─ What is possible/necessary/constrained/disposed?
│
├─ What causes/produces/makes a difference, by what mechanism?
│
├─ What constitutes/realizes/counts-as what?
│
└─ How is any of this observed/evidenced/represented/modeled?

Every claim additionally declares its legitimate dependence/scope coordinates.
```

This grammar is deliberately **pre-ontological** in the sense that it does not claim Reality contains six boxes. It is the smallest WDF0 research grammar that survived deletion without destructive collapse.

## 37. Why R1 wins

The most important finding is that WDF0 has silently depended on modal/causal vocabulary from the beginning.


Yet WDF0 only erected firewalls among these terms. It never researched their foundation deeply enough.

The cross-domain dependency is stronger than for physical geometry or semantics alone.

Therefore this residual meets the original WDF0 admission rule:
