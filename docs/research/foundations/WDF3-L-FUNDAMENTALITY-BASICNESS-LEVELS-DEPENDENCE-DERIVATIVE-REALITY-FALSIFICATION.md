# 2. The unary-property temptation

A common grammar writes:

```text

```

as if `fundamental` were an intrinsic unary property.

But most philosophical analyses immediately invoke a relation:

```text

y is derivative from what?

```

### L suspicion

```text
Fundamentality may be a status induced by a typed relation structure,
not primitive furniture attached to an entity.
```

# 4. Basicness relative to a relation

A target x is R-basic in scope S when no relevant y in S is R-prior to x under the admitted direction of R.

# 103. Final compressed judgment

WDF3-L eliminates the last major global hierarchy shortcut in the categorial architecture. `Fundamental`, `basic`, `independent`, `prior`, `lower-level`, and `derivative` do not name one universal order. Scale, composition, constitution, realization, formal derivability, historical lineage, semantic specification and institutional authority can all generate objective directional structure, but those directions need not agree and cannot be added into one principled scalar or total hierarchy.

Absolute fundamentality therefore cannot be treated as a primitive unary status without first selecting a privileged dependence/priority relation family. No cross-domain noncircular rule for selecting such a unique family survives. Ungroundedness presupposes grounding privilege; smallest-scale and highest-energy views fail outside—and do not automatically settle matters inside—physics; part-priority is contested by priority monism; explanatory, causal, historical, semantic and authority priority all pull apart. The safe reconstruction is relation-indexed basicness and derivativeness under a Typed Dependence/Priority Profile whose priority semantics themselves require licensing.

This reconstruction also dissolves several standard regress shortcuts. Strict set-theoretic well-foundedness is stronger than `having a metaphysical foundation`; infinite chains can coexist with weaker foundations, while genuine bottomless infinitism remains a coherent rival rather than an established actuality. Genuine dependence cycles likewise cannot be banned globally: many apparent cycles dissolve after relation typing, while coherentist/holist models leave open real cycles in some dependence relations. Foundationalism, infinitism and holism are therefore relation-specific rival structures, not World grammar axioms.

The result strengthens K rather than undermining it. A macro variable can be derivative-under realization while objectively real; a legal status derivative-under constitutive rules; a software service derivative-under implementation. The same target may be basic under another typed order. `DerivativeUnder_R != LessReal`, and some cross-order questions such as whether legal authority or physical substrate is `more fundamental` may be type errors until a comparison criterion is supplied.

After L, WDF3's surviving global architecture is extremely thin: scoped Reality claims, explicit criteria and CVEs, identity/individuation discipline, typed Reality relations, witnessed bridges, claim-relevance licenses, lifecycle/criterion-transition provenance, effective-reality loss/fiber discipline, and typed priority only when independently licensed. No universal Kind, ContentRole table, Grounding edge, Support relation, Emergence relation, Level hierarchy or Fundamentality order remains.

That degree of compression creates local closure pressure, but it is not yet a license to freeze WDF3. The next proper move is adversarial rather than constructive: search for alien, reflexive, stochastic, mathematical, quantum, synthetic and future Agent-era categories that were not central to building A→L, and try to break the entire architecture at once. Only if that succeeds without a new primitive or FoundationReopenCondition should WDF3 be considered for local foundation closure. That selects WDF3-M.
