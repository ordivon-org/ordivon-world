# 71. Final compressed judgment

WDF3-G eliminates persistence as a separate universal ontological relation. Strict persistence is simply strict numerical identity adjudicated across temporal cuts. The difficult substantive content lies not in a second `persists-as` relation but in the conditions that individuate one target through change. Those conditions vary across material objects, organisms, colonies, groups, institutions, software services and Agents, and no candidate support relation—material, spatiotemporal, causal, functional, organizational, informational, genealogical or institutional—survives as a universal sufficient persistence criterion.

This yields the Individuation-Profile Architecture. Any category or target used for counting, reidentification or persistence must expose the Reality-grounded conditions for synchronic distinction, diachronic persistence, tolerated replacement, interruption, birth/termination, branching, fusion and replication. The profile may be partial or contested; usefulness does not manufacture an ontology.

Fission, fusion and digital copying remain decisive. A predecessor cannot be strictly identical to two distinct forked successors, and two distinct predecessors cannot both be strictly identical to one merged successor. Checkpoint restore can preserve state or job lineage without preserving process identity; a service or institutional role may persist across realizer/occupant gaps; a process token generally does not. Thus lifecycle reasoning requires typed relations such as successor, replica, split-from, merged-from, restore-from and strict-identity-continuation, with graph reachability never standing in for identity.

The largest architectural consequence is compression into Classification/Kind. Persistence is now best treated as the diachronic facet of individuation attached to an admitted category/target, not as a peer WDF3 content role. This puts maximal pressure on the final surviving global content-role distinction: Characterization versus Classification/Kind. WDF3-H must decide whether Kind is a genuinely irreducible Reality role, a validity-bounded local ontology pattern, or a reconstructible package of characterization, individuation, modality, history and constitution.
