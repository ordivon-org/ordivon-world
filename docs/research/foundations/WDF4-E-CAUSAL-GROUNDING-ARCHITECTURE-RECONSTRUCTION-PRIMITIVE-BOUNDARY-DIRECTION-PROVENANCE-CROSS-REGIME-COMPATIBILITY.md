# 56. Final compressed judgment

WDF4-E solves the architectural problem left by D without pretending to solve ultimate causal metaphysics.

The causal foundation no longer needs three parallel grounding objects. CGD, Orientation Provenance and Primitive Commitment Boundary are compressed into one Grounding Commitment Profile. A GCP states which causal projection is being grounded, which theory family is invoked, whether the theory is reductive/nonreductive/mixed, where primitive commitment enters, what orients the causal relation, how model variables map to Reality, which CVE applies and what would falsify the grounding.

This allows TCSA-gamma to support multiple legitimate grounding theories for the same causal claim. Process and intervention accounts can be complementary: process grounds actual realization while intervention/invariance grounds modal influence. Powers, laws or primitive causal relations can supply alternative metaphysical interpretations. Agreement among them strengthens the causal claim but does not prove metaphysical identity.

E therefore rejects one overloaded notion of `grounding equivalence`. Observational equivalence, interventional equivalence, causal-query equivalence, grounding-profile equivalence and metaphysical equivalence are distinct. Two theories can be causally concordant yet metaphysically incompatible; they can be complementary rather than equivalent; they can agree only within one query/CVE; or they can remain empirically underdetermined. Grounding compatibility is correspondingly typed rather than scalar.

Causal abstraction gives this architecture a concrete formal bridge discipline. High- and low-level causal models can preserve interventions exactly or approximately under explicit maps and tolerances. This supports objectively valid macro/institution/software/Agent causal variables without requiring identity with microphysical variables or an extra macro force. But preservation of total intervention effects does not automatically preserve token actual-cause judgments; ACV transport requires preservation of the actual-path, contrast, normality and preemption structure relevant to that query.

The cross-regime reconstruction is successful. Physical claims can combine process, intervention and nomic profiles. Biological claims can combine perturbational MIP and mechanistic ARP. Institutional claims can preserve constitutive status changes separately from behavioral causation. Software claims can combine execution traces with replay/fault-injection evidence and causal abstraction. Agent chains can compose semantic influence, decision, authority, execution and downstream causal segments without flattening authority or responsibility into causal production.

The result is a robust common causal grammar without a forced common metaphysical primitive:

Reality target

+ model-Reality bridge
