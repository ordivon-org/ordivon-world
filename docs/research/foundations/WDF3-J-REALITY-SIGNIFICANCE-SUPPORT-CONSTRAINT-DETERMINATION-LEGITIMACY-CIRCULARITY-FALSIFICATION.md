# 3. Correlation-without-support battery

A correlation can be real and repeatable while failing to supply the relevant determination relation.

## C1 — Barometer / storm

Barometer readings correlate with storms because atmospheric pressure participates in the common causal structure.

The barometer reading does not thereby causally generate the storm.

It can be excellent evidence while lacking the claimed causal direction.

## C2 — Rooster / sunrise

Repeated temporal correlation does not make rooster crowing a support basis for solar motion.

## C3 — Proxy variable

Postal code can predict disease risk because of latent socioeconomic/environmental structure.

The postal code itself need not be the disease-producing mechanism.

## C4 — Dataset label leakage

A file naming convention can perfectly predict a class in a dataset while being unrelated to the target phenomenon outside the data pipeline.

### Result

```text
Correlation = possible SEI
Correlation != typed support relation
```

# 5. Mechanism battery: mere inclusion is not relevance

Mechanisms contain many parts, properties and activities.

Not every spatially contained part is constitutively relevant to the phenomenon under explanation.

Mechanistic philosophy explicitly treats **constitutive relevance** as a separate problem: which entities/activities/organizational features contribute to the phenomenon rather than merely being present?

### Minimal counterexample

```text
component x is physically inside system S
S exhibits phenomenon psi
x has no difference-making/constitutive contribution to psi
```

Then:

```text
Inside(x,S) != ConstitutivelyRelevant(x,psi)
```

### Result

```text
MereParthood != Support
```

# 93. Final compressed judgment

WDF3-J eliminates `support` as the next candidate pseudo-primitive. Correlation, causal influence, constitution, law, structural entailment, genealogy, function, specification conformance and institutional authority can all make a Reality-significance claim legitimate, but they do so through different typed relations. Calling all of them `support` destroys precisely the direction, failure conditions and composition behavior needed to distinguish genuine relevance from proxy correlation, mere inclusion, irrelevant lawfulness, dead specifications or invalid institutional rules.

The decisive reconstruction is Relation/Relevance/Evidence separation. First establish a typed Reality relation or an independently operative constitutive system. Then establish a Claim-Relevance License showing why that relation bears on the explicit target criterion and relevant contrast inside its CVE. Only afterwards may one use the informal language that the relation `supports` the classification. Evidence for the relation and relevance remains a third layer. This terminates the support-of-support regress in criterion semantics plus typed Reality facts rather than in a new universal metaphysical edge.

The gerrymandering attack also forces support coherence to be more subtle than one shared mechanism. Multiple realization, heterogeneous legal entry conditions and abstract functional kinds can have several distinct lower-level routes that converge on one non-enumerative criterion/consequence schema. What is forbidden is a bespoke per-member story whose only unity is the desired extension. Likewise, an enabling background shared by members and rivals is not classification support; contrastive relevance must identify what actually discriminates or constitutes the target claim.

Generic Grounding does not return. Even if grounding is treated as a coarse-grained genus over constitution and realization, it neither covers causal, historical, semantic and institutional relevance without distortion nor explains why a particular relation is relevant to a particular classification. `Building`, `Determination` and `Constraint` remain meta-family terms rather than inference operators. The universal survivor is again grammatical: typed relation, claim criterion, contrast, direction, CVE, relevance license, consequence, anti-circularity and evidence provenance.

This resolves the immediate conventionalism/support-circularity blocker sufficiently to expose the next hardest destructive test. Effective and emergent reality combines lossy coarse-graining, many-to-one realization, scale-bounded laws, macro autonomy and quotienting in exactly the way most likely to break TCRA/CRL. WDF3-K must therefore determine whether effective macro entities and variables can be Reality-significant without restoring primitive Kind, universal Levels or generic Grounding, and whether `RealitySignificant != Fundamental` survives genuine cross-scale cases.
