# 70. Final closeout judgment

WDF4-F successfully performs the independent destructive pass that E lacked.

The candidate does not survive unchanged merely because it was authored carefully. Two pieces are deleted or compressed: the broad GCP-alpha is reduced to a minimal Basis/Commitment/Scope/Failure profile; the enumerated GCR-alpha compatibility family is replaced by an orthogonal comparison tuple; CMRB-alpha is removed as an independently named object and folded into the general Reality-validation burden.

The four central burdens cannot be removed. Query typing is forced by the separation among causal effect, actual cause, mechanism, control and transport. The MIP/ARP distinction is forced from both sides: preemption and overdetermination preserve actual productive structure without simple net difference-making, while omission/double prevention and randomized effects preserve causal influence without ordinary positive productive transmission. Grounding disclosure is forced because intervention, process, mechanism, powers, laws and structural models place primitive causal commitment in different locations and causal direction does not ground itself. Reality validation is forced because internal causal-model semantics cannot by itself establish that real-world actions correspond to the model's interventions; exact, approximate and lossy causal abstractions likewise require explicit correspondence/error conditions.

Actual cause still does not require a third universal primitive. It remains a legitimate query-level verdict derived from causal projections, actual history, preemption/redundancy, contrast/normality where relevant, grain/CVE, grounding and validation. Removing actual-cause queries loses real token-selection work; promoting ActualCause to primitive adds no independently forced foundation content.

The candidate also survives the most dangerous conflict cases. Time-symmetric/retrocausal pressure can remain explicitly underdetermined rather than being overwritten by a past-to-future convention. Stochastic causal effects remain distinct from objective chance and token realization. Biological mechanism/effect disagreement becomes a typed diagnostic rather than a forced theory victory. Institutional and software higher-level causes survive through validated causal abstraction without extra macro forces. Agent chains retain end-to-end causal analysis only through typed composition and never turn authority, semantic representation or responsibility into causal production.

The final result is therefore unusually thin relative to the research path that produced it. WDF4 Foundation v1 does not tell Reality what causation ultimately is. It specifies what a causal foundation must preserve in order not to destroy truth by conflation:

```text
Query
Projection
Grounding
Validation
```

and it freezes the firewalls required by the full A→F falsifier corpus.

No concrete WDF4-G residual survives F. The remaining open problems—objective chance/indeterminacy, category/rule genesis, regime transition, coupled-whole architecture, Time, Law and other Reality continents—are broader World problems, not unfinished causal-foundation subrounds.

Therefore WDF4 is closed locally and frozen.

Whole-World closure is not established, and the next World route must be selected afresh rather than inherited from the WDF4 residual history.
