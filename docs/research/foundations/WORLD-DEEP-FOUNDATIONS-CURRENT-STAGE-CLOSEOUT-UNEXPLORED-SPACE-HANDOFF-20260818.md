# 48. Final closeout claim

The correct conclusion is neither:

```text
“World foundations are incomplete, therefore prior research failed.”
```

nor:

```text
“WDF0–WDF2-N are deep, therefore World is complete.”
```

The defensible conclusion is:

> **Ordivon World now possesses a strong and unusually deep foundation in meta-ontology, modality and counterfactual architecture, but the current work demonstrates depth rather than whole-domain closure. Major pre-Agent theoretical continents—causation, lawhood, chance, powers, time, space, persistence, mereology, scale/emergence, dynamics, thermodynamics, quantum/spacetime physics, measurement, epistemic access, system boundaries, agency, institutional reality and others—remain unclosed or only indirectly touched. Agent-era realities add further potentially novel classes: machine-mediated observation/action, autonomous agent populations, programmable institutions, model-mediated feedback, copy/fork identity, recursive world-model interaction, synthetic/virtual worlds, machine-speed regime change, adversarial epistemology and human-agent hybrid systems. The previous local WDF2-N residual pointing toward effect/estimand research is therefore insufficient grounds for a global next-step commitment. The canonical next route and even its WDF identifier are reset to UNKNOWN. A new conversation must first rebuild a complete World coverage/residual map and let comparative falsification—not historical momentum—determine what deserves to become the next foundation.**
