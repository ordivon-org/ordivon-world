# 4. Rival A — Substance / Attribute / Relation Architecture

A traditional architecture takes something like:

```text
Substances / Objects
Attributes / Properties
Relations
Instantiation / Inherence
```

as fundamental or near-fundamental. A stronger Lowe-style version distinguishes:

```text
Objects
Modes
Kinds
Attributes
```

organized through instantiation and characterization relations.

## Reconstruction

```text
CHARACTERIZATION
  object instantiates attribute / bears mode

CLASSIFICATION / KIND
  object instantiates substantial universal / belongs to kind

OBTAINING / FACTIVITY
  requires instantiation fact/state-of-affairs or primitive truth relation

DEPENDENCE / PRIORITY
  substance independence + dependent modes;
  broader building relations must be added

REALIZATION / CONSTITUTION
  not native in the simplest architecture;
  requires additional relations
```

## Strengths

Strong for ordinary material objects and bearer/property reasoning. It naturally separates object/property, kind/attribute and particular/universal.

## Destructive pressure

Storms, flames, reactions, organisms and services carry identities through organized histories and constituent turnover; representing all of this as one substance with changing attributes pushes the real work into a hidden persistence theory.

Ownership, office, contract validity and authority require constitutive rule/history. Same-bytes/different-file and service migration require criterion-relative identity. Gauge/equivalence cases require independent difference-relevance criteria.

## Hidden imports

To cover the B battery the architecture tends to add:

```text
persistence criteria
facts/states of affairs
grounding/building relations
realization/constitution
institutional rules
invariance/equivalence criteria
```

## Verdict

```text
Excellent local architecture for many continuant/property regimes.
Not established as universal World architecture.
```

# 5. Rival B — Universal / Exemplification Architecture

Core:

```text
Particulars
Universals
Exemplification / Instantiation
```

A shared property is literally one universal instantiated by multiple particulars.

## Reconstruction

```text
CHARACTERIZATION
  direct: x exemplifies universal F

CLASSIFICATION / KIND
  kinds can be substantial universals or higher-order structures

OBTAINING
  exemplification becomes factive through state-of-affairs/fact machinery

DEPENDENCE
  requires additional essential/grounding relations

REALIZATION
  external to bare universal architecture
```

## Strength

Very strong for objective repeatability and shared characterization.

## Failures

Historical species, institutional kinds and specification-defined software kinds do not reduce cleanly to shared universals without importing history/rules/semantics.

`x exemplifies F` does not by itself solve truthmaking. Realization cannot be replaced by adding a universal `realizes-S` without hiding the realization semantics inside the predicate. Universals do not individuate concrete particulars or persistent entities by themselves.

## Hidden-import diagnosis

A universal architecture can encode every surviving role by introducing a universal corresponding to it, but this risks precisely the predicate-to-property reification that WDF3-A rejected.

## Verdict

```text
Strong theory of characterization/repeatability.
Insufficient as universal categorial compression.
```

# 6. Rival C — Trope / Mode Architecture

Core:

```text
particularized qualities / tropes / modes
resemblance/similarity structure
compresence/bundling relations
```

A red apple bears its own particular redness trope rather than sharing one universal redness.

## Reconstruction

```text
CHARACTERIZATION
  bearer has trope/mode

CLASSIFICATION / KIND
  resemblance classes / trope clusters / causal-historical groupings

OBTAINING
  tropes or trope complexes can serve as truthmakers

DEPENDENCE
  modes often depend on bearers; broader grounding must be added

REALIZATION
  organized trope complexes, but organization/role remains extra
```

## Strengths

Fine-grained local qualitative structure; avoids multiply located universals; tropes can be proposed as causal relata or truthmakers.

## Pressure

If objects are trope bundles, compresence must explain why these tropes form one object. If compresence is primitive, one-category parsimony weakens. Property change pressures bundle identity. Historical/institutional kinds require non-qualitative relations. Legal status cannot be reconstructed from intrinsic tropes alone.

## Verdict

```text
Strong local reconstruction of characterization and fine-grained truthmaking.
Fails as universal compression without importing compresence, history,
kind and constitutive structure.
```

# 7. Rival D — Bundle / Strict Nominalist / Resemblance Architecture

This family seeks severe parsimony through variants such as:

```text
Concrete particulars + resemblance
bundles/classes/sets of properties or tropes
or linguistic/conceptual classification
```

Strict nominalist versions reject special property entities.

## Reconstruction pressure

Characterization can be represented by resemblance, class membership or primitive predication, but:

```text
PredicateAvailability != CharacterizationTruth
ClassExtension != PropertyIdentity
```

Historical and institutional kinds are not mere resemblance classes. Bundle theories face identity pressure from indiscernible distinct objects and persistence through property change. Primitive true predication leaves the Reality-side truth condition underdescribed. Realization and noncausal determination require extra structure.

When resemblance, naturalness, eligibility or compresence become primitive, property-like architecture has often been reintroduced under another name.

## Verdict

```text
Real parsimony advantage at the entity-category level.
No global reconstruction of kind, factivity, realization and institutional determination.
```

# 8. Rival E — State-of-Affairs / Fact Architecture

Core candidate:

```text
particular + property/relation + exemplification tie
-> fact / obtaining state of affairs
```

## Reconstruction

```text
CHARACTERIZATION
  fact: a's being F

CLASSIFICATION / KIND
  fact: a's being K

OBTAINING
  native / strongest

DEPENDENCE
  facts can be relata of grounding

REALIZATION
  fact: base realizes role R
```

At first glance this architecture can encode all five roles.

## Encoding != explanation

Turning every claim into a fact does not explain:

```text
what properties/kinds are
what makes grounding different from causation
what makes realization different from representation
what binds object/property into one obtaining complex
```

Fact syntax packages the distinctions rather than deleting them.

Negative/general/disjunctive/modal truths pressure universal truthmaker architectures toward complex fact furniture or specialized truthmaking principles. Exemplification/tie theories also face binding-regress pressure unless the tie is treated internally or primitively.

## Verdict

```text
Best tournament architecture for explicit factivity/truthmaking.
Poor global compressor because it presupposes the other disputed roles.
```


Bare structural ontology risks violating:

```text
SharedFormalStructure != SharedOntology
```

Eliminative structural realism must explain what replaces relata; moderate structural realism retains entities/relations and therefore ceases to be a one-root compression. Institutional status also depends on rule/history, not arbitrary graph isomorphism.

## Verdict

```text
Strongest rival for physical relation/equivalence architecture.
Not established as universal World ontology.
```

# 42. Final compressed judgment

The architecture tournament does not justify replacing WDF3's uncertainty with Aristotle, universals, tropes, facts, processes, structures, grounding, or a one-category ontology. Every one captures a genuine part of the problem and provides strong local explanatory machinery, but each universalization either fails a B matched case or reintroduces missing distinctions through additional relations, histories, criteria, rules, truthmaking devices, realization mappings or invariance structures.

The strongest surviving global position is therefore neither ontological agnosticism nor unconstrained pluralism. It is **Global Minimalism / Local Pluralism**: World should freeze only the smallest cross-regime categorial discipline globally, while allowing objective local categories to be admitted when their criteria, Reality constraints, invariants, dependence/realization relations, cross-regime projections and deletion conditions survive falsification.

C also compresses CCG-beta further. Quantity, absence, occurrence, identity and equivalence were already demoted in B. C now demotes realization/constitution into a typed determination/building family and factivity from a peer category into a global truth/satisfaction coordinate. The only content roles still resisting global deletion are characterization and classification/kind; even these are not asserted to be fundamental furniture.

The next problem is the one capable of falsifying the entire reconstruction: whether principled local category admission can be made non-circular, non-relativistic and compositionally coherent across regime boundaries. That is WDF3-D.
