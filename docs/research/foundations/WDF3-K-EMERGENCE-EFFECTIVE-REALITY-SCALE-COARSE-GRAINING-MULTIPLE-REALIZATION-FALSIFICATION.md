# 98. Final compressed judgment

WDF3-K finds no need for a primitive `Emergence` relation. Effective and emergent phenomena fragment into scale-indexed coarse-graining, realization, constitution, statistical aggregation, nomic autonomy, multiple realization, approximation and typed causal/specification/institutional relations. `Micro` and `macro` are themselves indexed by scale, grain or abstraction relation rather than universal ontological levels.

The crucial asymmetry is between macro-identical/micro-different and the reverse. Many distinct detailed states can legitimately map to one macro state; this is the ordinary structure of coarse-graining and multiple realization. But two genuinely identical complete relevant base states, evaluated under the same criterion, bridge and CVE, cannot deterministically produce different macro adjudications. Apparent counterexamples reveal omitted history, institutional context, authority, boundary conditions or scale semantics. This yields the Same-Base Adjudication Constraint while explicitly avoiding a theorem of instantaneous microphysical supervenience.

RG, critical phenomena and EFT show how objective effective structure can be scale-bounded. Different microscopic details can become irrelevant under RG flow; low-energy EFTs can omit heavy degrees and remain controlled and predictive within their validity domain. These facts support `Effective != Fictional` and `ScaleRelative != Subjective`, but not `Effective = Fundamental`. Autonomy, universality, decoupling and predictive closure are strong evidence/relevance relations, not proofs of ontological independence.

The architecture survives lossy coarse-graining only after adding fiber semantics. A macro variable must be robust across the micro distinctions its mapping intentionally discards, relative to a target claim, perturbation family, error tolerance and CVE. Approximate closure is acceptable; exact closure is not required. Rival coarse-grainings must be challenged rather than assuming a preferred macro partition from convenience. Multiply realized variables require per-family typed bridge/relevance witnesses converging on a non-enumerative macro criterion.

Strong emergence, generic downward causation, universal supervenience, universal Levels and generic Grounding are not required by any tested case. A novel effective law or interaction term does not by itself imply a new fundamental force. A virtual machine, institution, organism-level function or Agent service can be derivative and nevertheless objectively real when its operative semantics, identity criteria, bridges and consequences are independently established.

The resulting architecture makes the distinction `RealitySignificant != Fundamental` unavoidable. WDF3 can now explain how derivative effective reality is admitted, but it has not yet reconstructed `fundamental`, `basic`, `more fundamental than`, or `derivative` themselves. Since scale, constitution, realization, causal priority and explanatory priority do not obviously form one order, fundamentality/basicness becomes the next upstream blocker. That selects WDF3-L.
