## 5. Meta-rules

### Dependence disclosure

A claim should expose relevant dependencies such as:

```text
Time / History
Reference frame
Scale / grain
Boundary / system criterion
Actor / outcome
Institution / rules
Observer / sensor / path
Measurement procedure
Model / question / tolerance
```

WDF0 v1 does not freeze a production DependenceSignature schema. It freezes the research discipline that hidden dependencies are not to be treated as context-free absolutes.

### Scoped objectivity

Relative claims can be objective once legitimate dependencies are explicit and the claim remains Reality-constrained under the transformations/tests that should be irrelevant.

### Partial world models

A partial model is legitimate when its scope, uncertainty, currentness, evidence and reopen/model-revision conditions are explicit.

### Structural revisability

General world learning must permit revision of variables, relations, boundaries, mechanisms, scales and categories rather than only updating state/probability inside a fixed schema.

## 8. WDF0 reopen conditions

Reopen WDF0 only if later work shows at least one of:

1. the compressed root grammar cannot express a major Reality domain without destructive loss;
2. a hard anti-collapse law is falsified by strong cross-domain evidence;
3. typed dependence/scoping cannot handle a major objective relational fact;
4. a single-root ontology reconstructs the WDF0 distinctions without hidden imports;
5. a concrete case requires strong ontological emergence/new fundamental macro law;
6. the Reality/representation firewall itself proves fundamentally mistaken.

Otherwise later findings extend WDF1+ or domain foundations rather than reopening WDF0.
