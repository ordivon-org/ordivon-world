# 14. Realization composition

Multiple-realization literature shows that one realized kind/property may admit many distinct realizers, and mechanistic accounts distinguish flat from dimensioned realization.

Consider:

```text
molecular organization M realizes cellular function C
C participates in / realizes organ function O
```

Can we infer:

```text
M realizes O?
```

Sometimes perhaps, but only if:

```text
C's contribution to O is constitutively sufficient in the relevant organization,
role semantics align,
CVE overlaps,
and no omitted organizational context is required.
```

If O depends on interactions among many cells/tissues, the local M alone does not realize O.

### Result

```text
Realization is NOT generically transitive.
```

### Conditional rule

```text
Realizes(a,b)
Realizes(b,c)
+ RoleClosure(a,b,c)
+ OrganizationPreserved
+ CVEOverlap
=> CandidateRealizes(a,c)
```

The conclusion remains case-specific and requires a composition witness.

# 31. BCW-alpha — Bridge Composition Witness

Two bridges may compose only with an explicit:

# **Bridge Composition Witness — BCW-alpha**

For:

```text
B1: A -R1-> B
B2: B' -R2-> C
```

composition requires at least:

```text
1. NodeAlignment
   B and B' are same/compatible under explicit identity/translation criterion.

2. CVEOverlap
   relevant validity envelopes overlap for the claimed path.

3. RoleCompatibility
   R1 and R2 belong to a licensed composition pattern.

4. CriterionCompatibility
   downstream criterion does not depend on distinctions already discarded upstream.

5. InvariantPreservation
   all invariants required by the target claim survive B1 and B2.

6. LossSafety
   upstream loss does not destroy a distinction required by B2 or C.

7. BackgroundCompatibility
   authority, boundary, scale, time, environment and rule conditions are jointly satisfiable.

8. Directionality
   neither relation is being silently inverted.

9. Factivity
   both bridge claims actually obtain; representation/model edges are not mistaken for ontic edges.

10. DerivedBridgeType
    the composition's truth role is stated rather than guessed.
```

Only then may one assert:

```text
Compose(B1,B2 | BCW) => B3
```

# 36. Does a generic Grounding relation earn admission?

E tests the strongest generic candidate:

```text

```

as a common abstraction over:

```text
constitution
realization

institutional determination
perhaps truthmaking
```

Potential benefit:

```text

```

But the bridge battery exposes three costs.

## Cost G1 — Different composition laws

Material constitution, realization, parthood and institutional determination do not share one demonstrated transitivity/composition law.

## Cost G2 — Different failure conditions

A realization can fail through role mismatch; institutional constitution through invalid authority/rules; composition through missing parts/organization; truthmaking through factivity mismatch.

## Cost G3 — Different remediation/evidence

Evidence needed to establish each relation is materially different.

Therefore a generic Grounding edge would delete precisely the truth-role information needed for safe bridge composition.

### Verdict

```text
Generic Grounding as first-class cross-regime edge = NOT ADMITTED
```

# 56. Final compressed judgment

WDF3-E rejects the idea that cross-regime Reality can be connected by one metaphysical arrow. Realization, constitution, parthood, implementation, representation, abstraction, projection, reduction, causation, historical derivation and institutional mediation have materially different truth roles, failure conditions, evidential bases and composition behavior. Some typed relations can compose under fixed semantics; some compose only with explicit role/invariant preservation; some support only a path-level dependence story; and some, especially representation, are non-transitive by default.

The decisive reconstruction is proof-carrying bridge composition. Every bridge requires a Typed Bridge Witness, and every inferred composite cross-regime claim requires a Bridge Composition Witness establishing node alignment, validity-envelope overlap, relation-role compatibility, preservation of downstream-required distinctions, invariant transport, compatible background conditions, directionality and factivity. A path is not a direct edge.

Generic Grounding does not solve this problem. It would erase the very differences needed to know whether a bridge chain composes, and grounding's own formal properties are themselves disputed. `Building` therefore survives only as a research-family label, not as an inference operator.

The strongest remaining pressure is now unavoidable: TBW/BCW safety depends on deciding which differences matter under transformations. That problem appears simultaneously in gauge equivalence, coarse-graining, multiple realization, implementation refinement, service/Agent persistence, institutional succession and category lifecycle. Therefore the residual does not select Grounding or Emergence next. It selects WDF3-F — Categorial Identity / Difference-Relevance / Invariance Under Transformation.
