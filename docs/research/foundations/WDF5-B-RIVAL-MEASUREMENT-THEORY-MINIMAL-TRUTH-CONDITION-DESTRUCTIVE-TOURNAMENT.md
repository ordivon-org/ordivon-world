# 2. Important typing correction discovered during B

A major portion of philosophical disagreement was found to arise because the word `measurement` is used for several different truth roles.

B therefore separates:

```text
MeasurementProcedure / MeasurementAttempt
MeasurementEvent / Execution
MeasurementResultAttribution
ValidMeasurementAttribution
MeasurementQuality
```

These are not identical.

A procedure can be designed and executed as a measurement procedure while the result is inaccurate, biased, poorly validated or even invalid for the intended target.

Therefore:

```text
ProcedureExists
!= ValidMeasurementResult
```

and:

```text
MeasurementQualityCondition
!= MeasurementProcessIdentity by default.
```

This distinction resolves many false disputes over whether calibration, uncertainty, reliability or validation are "necessary for measurement".

They may be necessary for specified quality/validity claims without being constitutive of every measurement attempt/process.

# 3. Rival family T1 — Representational / axiomatic measurement theory

## Strongest version

Measurement/quantification is grounded by preserving relevant empirical relational structure in a formal/numerical representation.

Strong contributions:

```text
scale structure matters
not every numeric mapping is meaningful
empirical relations constrain admissible transformations
quantitative operations require justified structure
```

## Destructive pressure

A naive version presupposes that the empirical qualitative structure can be independently recovered and then mapped to numbers.

Model-based critiques show that empirical structure and quantity identification are often theory/model dependent.

The same data may support different quantity models.

Psychometric constructs rarely arrive with a fully pre-given empirical concatenation/order structure.

Software metrics may have precisely defined numerical structure while targeting the wrong operational attribute.

Thus:

```text
StructurePreservingNumericalRepresentation
!= SufficientMeasurementCondition
```

and:

```text
ExplicitPreGivenEmpiricalRelationalStructure
!= UniversalNecessaryCondition
```

## Surviving residue

```text
Value / Scale / Comparison Semantics
```

survives strongly.

If outputs are treated as quantity/property values, the meaning of order/difference/ratio/equality/comparison operations must be declared and justified.

### T1 disposition

```text
COMPRESSED TO PARTIAL SUBSYSTEM
NOT COMPLETE MEASUREMENT THEORY
```

# 4. Rival family T2 — Operationalist / conventionalist

## Strongest version

Measurement terms gain empirical content through specified operations/procedures/conventions.

Strength:

```text
forces operational realizability
prevents unconstrained metaphysical quantity talk
makes repeatable procedures explicit
```

## Destructive pressure

If target identity is exhausted by the chosen operation, validity becomes circular:

```text
X = whatever procedure P produces
therefore P always measures X
```

Multiple legitimate procedures can measure the same intended target while differing operationally.

One procedure can also produce stable outputs while tracking a nuisance variable rather than the intended construct.

Institutional targets can be rule-constituted without being identical to one data-collection procedure.

Therefore:

```text
OperationalDefinition != TargetIdentity
```

and:

```text
ProcedureSpecification
!= SufficientMeasurementCondition
```

## Surviving residue

Some concrete:

```text
Procedure / Method / Realization chain
```

is needed for empirical measurement practice, but this can be indirect, model-mediated and distributed across multiple acquisition/computation stages.

### T2 disposition

```text
STRONG OPERATIONALISM REJECTED
PROCEDURAL REALIZATION ROLE SURVIVES
```

# 5. Rival family T3 — Realist quantity / true-value accounts

## Strongest version

Measurement attributes values to mind-independent or otherwise objectively scoped target properties/quantities.

This captures the intuition that measurement is not arbitrary coding.

## Destructive pressure

WDF5-A already falsified universal assumptions that:

```text
every target is a natural physical quantity
every target has exactly one unique true scalar value
constructed/institutional quantities are unreal
```

Physical metrology itself allows definitional uncertainty and sets/ranges of true values under some descriptions.

Latent psychological constructs and institution-relative quantities can be objective under declared criteria/CVE without being fundamental natural magnitudes.

Therefore:

```text
NaturalPhysicalQuantityExistence
!= UniversalNecessaryCondition
```

and:

```text
UniqueTrueScalarValue
!= UniversalNecessaryCondition
```

## Surviving residue

Measurement still requires **target-directed attribution**.

A result must purport to be about some declared target attribute/property/quantity/construct rather than merely be a number generated by a procedure.

Thus:

```text
TargetAttribution
```

survives strongly, while heavy realist metaphysics is demoted to regime-specific grounding.

### T3 disposition

```text
THIN REALIST TARGET-DIRECTEDNESS SURVIVES
STRONG TRUE-VALUE REALISM REJECTED AS UNIVERSAL
```

# 6. Rival family T4 — Uncertainty-oriented metrology

## Strongest version

Measurement results are value attributions accompanied by uncertainty and model/reference structure rather than simple revelation of an exact true value.

This is extremely strong for mature physical metrology.

## Destructive pressure

Many ordinary/scientific measurement results are reported without explicit uncertainty budgets.

The absence of an uncertainty statement may make a result incomplete or low-quality for a use without making the process cease to be measurement practice.

Psychometric validity problems can persist even with well-characterized statistical uncertainty.

A wrong-target proxy can have a beautifully estimated uncertainty distribution.

Therefore:

```text
ExplicitUncertaintyStatement
!= UniversalNecessaryConditionForMeasurementProcess
```

and:

```text
KnownUncertainty
!= SufficientMeasurementValidity
```

## Surviving residue

Uncertainty is a major:

```text
quality / limitation / result-characterization layer
```

and is likely mandatory for strong validity/fitness claims in many regimes.

### T4 disposition

```text
DEMOTED FROM DEFINITION TO QUALITY/RESULT ARCHITECTURE
```

# 7. Rival family T5 — Model-based measurement

## Strongest version

Measurement outcomes are generated through models of the measurement process; indications/data do not interpret themselves.

Calibration and quantity individuation require theoretical/statistical assumptions, model prediction and coherence across procedures.

Strong result:

```text
Indication -> target value
```

is rarely a theory-free primitive.

## Destructive pressure

An explicit mathematical measurement model is not always documented.

Simple counting/comparison/procedural measurements may rely on tacit or conventional mappings rather than explicit equations.

Historical functionalist cases show measurement practice can precede shared mature theory of the target.

Therefore:

```text
ExplicitMathematicalMeasurementModel
!= UniversalNecessaryCondition
```

and:

```text
SharedMatureTargetTheory
!= UniversalNecessaryCondition
```

Model fit is also not sufficient: a model can accurately predict a proxy while the interpretation as the intended target is wrong.

## Surviving residue

The stronger abstract survivor is:

```text
Attribution / Interpretation Bridge
```

There must be some warranted rule/model/calibration/comparison/inference structure connecting empirical indications or upstream measured inputs to the value/status attributed to the target.

The bridge need not always be explicit mathematics.

### T5 disposition

```text
ONE OF THE STRONGEST PARTIAL SURVIVORS
EXPLICIT MODEL DEMOTED
ATTRIBUTION BRIDGE RETAINED
```

# 8. Rival family T6 — Relational / structural / traceability accounts

## Strongest version

Measurement is fundamentally relational: objects/results are compared through measuring systems/reference structures, and traceability supports wider networks of comparability.

This explains why:

```text
same numeric format != meaningful comparability
```

and why practical measurement networks require reference coherence.

## Destructive pressure

External standards are not universally necessary for local calibration/measurement.

Some measurements are meaningful within local or conventional scales before insertion into a global traceability network.

Psychometric and institutional quantities may rely on population norms, experimental manipulations or versioned definitions rather than physical etalons.

Therefore:

```text
ExternalStandardTraceability
!= UniversalNecessaryCondition
```

and:

```text
GlobalComparability
!= NecessaryForEveryLocalMeasurement
```

## Surviving residue

Some form of:

```text
Value / Reference / Comparison Semantics
```

remains difficult to delete.

A result interpreted as a value must have rules for what sameness/difference/order/reference means in context.

### T6 disposition

```text
GLOBAL TRACEABILITY DEFINITION REJECTED
REFERENCE/COMPARISON SEMANTICS SURVIVES
```

# 9. Rival family T7 — Information-theoretic accounts

## Strongest version

A measuring system/result must carry information about the target; its output must discriminate relevant target states/values rather than be independent of them.

This captures a deep necessity intuition:

```text
if output is completely insensitive to target differences,
it cannot measure that target.
```

## Destructive pressure

Mutual information/correlation alone is not sufficient.

Examples:

```text
hospital readmission correlates with quality but is not identical to quality
clicks correlate with engagement
skin conductance correlates with multiple latent states
location correlates with socioeconomic status
```

A proxy can carry high information about a target while remaining an invalid target attribution for the intended claim.

Information can also be carried by purely predictive or classificatory systems without those outputs being measurements of the named target.

Therefore:

```text
InformationAboutTarget
!= SufficientMeasurementCondition
```

## Surviving residue

A broader condition survives strongly:

```text
Target-Relevant Empirical Sensitivity / Discriminability
```

The empirical grounding chain must respond appropriately to relevant differences in the target/target conditions under the declared scope.

### T7 disposition

```text
INFORMATION-ONLY THEORY REJECTED
TARGET SENSITIVITY SURVIVES
```

# 10. Rival family T8 — Causal interaction / detection accounts

## Destructive pressure

Indirect measurement can infer a target from upstream measured quantities through theory/model/calculation.

Remote astronomical quantities, derived quantities and some institution/population statistics are not captured by one direct target-instrument causal edge.

BIPM annotations explicitly allow "experimentally obtaining" to include models and calculations grounded in theoretical considerations.

Furthermore, causal interaction is not sufficient:


Therefore:

```text
DirectCausalInteraction
```

```text
CausalInteraction
```

## Surviving residue

What survives is broader:


The attributed result must ultimately connect to target-relevant empirical acquisition/interaction/observation records, directly or through valid derivation.

# 11. Rival family T9 — Construct-validity / psychometric accounts

## Strongest version

For latent or theory-mediated constructs, score production is insufficient; observable variables must be validly connected to the intended construct.

Reliability and quantitative precision do not establish construct validity.

## Destructive pressure

Explicit validity evidence is epistemic support for a claim; it is not identical to the truthmaker of the measurement relation.

A measurement attribution could in principle be correct even before scientists possess strong validation evidence.

Conversely, extensive but misleading validation evidence does not make a false target attribution true.

Thus:

```text
ValidityEvidence
!= ConstitutiveMeasurementTruth by identity
```

## Surviving residue

The ontology-level survivor is:

```text
Attribution Bridge Adequacy
```

The observable/result-to-target interpretation must in fact be adequate within scope.

Evidence/validation practices are how Agents justify or test that adequacy.

### T9 disposition

```text
STRONG LATENT-TARGET FALSIFIER
VALIDITY-EVIDENCE DEMOTED FROM TRUTHMAKER
BRIDGE ADEQUACY RETAINED
```

Therefore:



## Surviving residue

# 52. Minimal truth-condition residual MTR-B1

After deleting stronger conditions, B leaves the following provisional residual for **Valid Measurement Attribution**, not for every measurement attempt/procedure.

```text
MTR-B1

A result R is a candidate ValidMeasurementAttribution
of value/status V to attribute A of target T only if:

M1 Target Attribution
   R explicitly or implicitly attributes V to A(T),
   under a sufficiently specified TargetCriterion.

M2 Target-Relevant Empirical Grounding Chain
   R is grounded in an empirical acquisition/evaluation chain
   relevant to the target instance/conditions,
   directly or through valid upstream measurements/records.

M3 Attribution / Interpretation Bridge
   there exists a warranted rule/model/calibration/comparison/
   inference structure connecting the empirical basis to V as a
   value/status of A(T), not merely as a predictor/correlate/proxy.

M4 Value / Comparison Semantics
   V inhabits a declared result structure specifying the admissible
   meaning of identity/difference/order/reference/comparison operations
   needed by the measurement claim.

M5 Scope / CVE
   the attribution is evaluated under declared conditions/grain/version/time,
   inherited from WDF3 rather than introduced as a new WDF5 primitive.
```

This is a **minimal residual**, not a frozen definition.

# 54. Why M2 cannot be deleted

Thus M2 survives strongly.

# 55. Why M3 cannot be deleted

M3 is what separates:

```text

correlating with A(T)
causing A(T)
```

from legitimately attributing a value/status as a measurement of A(T).

Thus M3 is the strongest WDF5-specific candidate burden.

# 56. Why M4 cannot yet be deleted

If a result is said to be a value/status but no semantics specify what equality/order/difference/reference means, then arbitrary coding and measurement are indistinguishable.

However B does **not** establish that M4 must always be a numerical scale.

Thus:
