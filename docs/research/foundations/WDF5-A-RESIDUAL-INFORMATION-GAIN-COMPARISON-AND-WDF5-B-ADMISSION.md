```text

```

Analytical-chemistry work also shows comparability need not always mean simple traceability to SI base units; reference structures can differ by measurement domain.

## Weakness

Reference architecture is downstream of the truth-condition problem.

```text

```

Therefore freezing Reference first would prejudge a live dispute.

# 18. Specific rivalry pressures already established

## Representational vs model-based

Representational theories emphasize structure-preserving numerical representation.

Model-based critiques argue that the empirical structure itself cannot simply be inferred independently of theory/model assumptions, and that measurement outcomes are produced by inference within idealized measurement-process models.

Therefore:

```text
StructurePreservationAlone
is not yet established as sufficient measurement grounding.
```

## Model-based vs functionalist

Model-based accounts emphasize theoretical/statistical models.

Historical functionalist pressure shows scientific communities can treat measurement as epistemically privileged even before convergence on a shared theory of the target quantity.

Therefore:

```text
SharedMatureTargetTheory
is not established as necessary.
```

## Metrological traceability vs model-based calibration

Formal metrology strongly values traceability for large-scale comparability.

Model-based calibration work argues measurement standards are not strictly necessary for successful calibration.

Therefore:

```text
TraceabilityToExternalStandard
is not established as necessary for all measurement.
```

## Psychometric validity vs simple score/reliability theories

Latent construct measurement requires a bridge from observable task output to the intended construct.

Therefore:

```text
ReliableQuantitativeOutput
is not sufficient.
```
