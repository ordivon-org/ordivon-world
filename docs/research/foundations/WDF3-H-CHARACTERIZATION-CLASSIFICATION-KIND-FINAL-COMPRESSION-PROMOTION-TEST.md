# 74. Final compressed judgment

WDF3-H falsifies the last fixed global ContentRole inherited from WDF3-C. The earlier `Characterization != Classification/Kind` firewall remains correct against naive current-property-bundle theories, but it does not establish an irreducible Kind relation. Chemistry, biology, artifacts, software, institutions and Agent-era roles all supply cases where kind membership diverges from current qualitative appearance; in every tested case, however, the missing truth can be carried by an explicit Reality-grounded package of criterion, structure, history, individuation, modality, constitution, realization, specification or authority. Conversely, large characterization changes can preserve classification when those changes are explicitly irrelevant under the admitted package.

Kind therefore survives as a useful and often objectively real **derived classification**, not as globally primitive World furniture. A kind label becomes a handle for a validated classification package; classification is the adjudication of a target against that package. This is not nominalism or eliminativism: institutional statuses, chemical elements or biological lineages can remain genuinely Reality-significant even when their membership truth is reconstructible rather than primitive.

The compression also removes the need for a fixed global ContentRole enum. World requires a scoped structured Reality claim with explicit criteria, factivity, validity, individuation and bridge/lifecycle provenance when relevant; local theories determine the typed content. `UniversalClassificationOperation != UniversalClassificationOntology` now joins the earlier WDF3 results `UniversalResearchGrammar != UniversalRealityFurniture` and `UniversalBridgeGrammar != UniversalBridgeOntology`.

But this successful reduction exposes the hardest remaining categorial problem. Explicit criteria are cheap. Arbitrary disjunctions can define perfectly factual classes. Therefore criterion explicitness, factual extension and usefulness are insufficient to establish ontic significance. WDF3 still lacks a principled answer to why `chemical element`, a biological lineage or an institutional office can deserve Reality-significant classification while a gerrymandered union of unrelated objects does not. That naturalness/objective-carving problem is now the upstream blocker for classification admission, equivalence naturalness and WDF3 closure. It selects WDF3-I.
