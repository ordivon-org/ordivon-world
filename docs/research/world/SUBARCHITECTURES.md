# World-Internal Subarchitectures

World contains locally frozen subarchitectures without claiming that each is a first-layer universal primitive.

## Causal Reality Architecture — WDF4

Purpose: provide typed semantics for causal questions and claims while keeping causation distinct from correlation, temporal priority, constitution, constraint, authority, responsibility, and mere model dependence.

Foundation standing: **WDF4 Foundation v1 FROZEN; LocalFoundationClosure established; WDF4-G not admitted.**

Detailed evidence starts at [`../foundations/WDF4-A-CAUSAL-REALITY-CURRENT-USE-TERM-SEPARATION-HIDDEN-ASSUMPTION-INVENTORY.md`](../foundations/WDF4-A-CAUSAL-REALITY-CURRENT-USE-TERM-SEPARATION-HIDDEN-ASSUMPTION-INVENTORY.md) and closes in the WDF4 A→F closeout linked from [`FOUNDATIONS.md`](FOUNDATIONS.md).

## Property Evaluation Architecture — WDF5

Purpose: separate target actuality and attribution from evaluation/value structure, encoding, cross-evaluation comparability, transport, quality, uncertainty, and decision use.

Foundation standing: **WDF5 Foundation v1 FROZEN; LocalFoundationClosure established; WDF5-J not admitted.**

Historical route names around Measurement / Quantification / Reference / Comparability are preserved, but the canonical owner referent is **Property Evaluation Architecture**.

## Temporal Structure Architecture — TSA

Purpose: own World-level temporal **target-structure semantics** without taking over physical spacetime realization, Runtime scheduling, Network clocks, provider timestamps, or domain-local temporal mechanisms.

Foundation standing: **TSAF0 FROZEN; local closure established; TSAF1 unknown/not admitted; no global WDF number assigned.**

TSA numbering is local by design. Do not infer that the absence of a WDF number makes TSAF0 less foundational, or that a future WDF6 must be temporal.

## Numbering rule

`WDF4`, `WDF5`, and `TSAF0` identify canonical research/admission lineage. They do not encode ontology depth, owner priority, or the next research route.
