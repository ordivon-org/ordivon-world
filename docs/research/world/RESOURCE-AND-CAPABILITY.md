# Resource and Capability Research — Current World Standing

This is the **current first lookup** for the World-owned Resource and Actor × Environment × Institution capability programmes. It is a compact recovery/publication surface over the detailed R0–R10 and CF0–CF9 evidence retained in `docs/`; it does not turn either programme into a production registry, planner, service, or global ontology.

## Current standing

| Programme | Standing | Current production disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Resource Ontology R0–R10 | **COMPLETE / CLOSED** | semantic doctrine retained; generic `resource_discovery` / `ResourceOpportunityBoard` executable planning layer retired in the 2026-08-27 deletion tournament after no natural production consumer remained |
| Capability Field CF0–CF9 | **COMPLETE / CLOSED** | causal/query-relative doctrine retained; **0** Capability Field/Environment/Institution production objects or services admitted |

The two programmes converge on one owner-preserving rule:

> Keep Reality truth with the owner that can reconcile it. Project Resource, Actionability, Option and effective Capability only for the actor/transition/demand/time relation that needs them. Promote shared machinery only after repeated missing executable responsibility survives owner-local resolution.

## Resource R0–R10 survivors

The canonical compression is [`../../resource-option-capability-model.md`](../../resource-option-capability-model.md), backed by [`../../resource-ontology-r10-compression-promotion.md`](../../resource-ontology-r10-compression-promotion.md). The strongest retained laws are:

- **boundary-relative enrollment** — Resource truth depends on actor/system boundary, Reality aspect and a supported transition;
- **native truth, projected relation** — World may join owner-native facts but does not become a second source authority;
- **claim-bound evidence** — candidate labels, discovery metadata and historical receipts do not self-promote into current capability truth;
- **history is not currentness** — applicability is recomputed claim-by-claim/as-of rather than stored as one universal lifecycle bit;
- **Actionability and Option are downstream** — existence, authority, access, demand fit and selectability remain distinct;
- **feasibility is conjunctive and owner-local** — requirements/assignments/constraints do not earn a global composition engine;
- **quantity only when binding** — money, rate, quota, stock, time, attention and capacity remain native-unit constraints rather than a universal scalar;
- **substitution/redundancy are contextual** — candidate count or superficial diversity is not resilience proof;
- **Action / Effect / Outcome / Attribution / Knowledge remain separate**;
- **evidence acquisition is decision-scoped**;
- **promote semantics before machinery**.

### Production consequence after the 2026-08-27 deletion tournament

The former `src/ordivon_world/resource_discovery.py` planning model had become a compatibility-only executable surface: repository-wide current-code search found no production consumer outside its own tests/facade compatibility, while Finance, Game, Studio/Media, Security and Human already owned their real reducers/admission/decision semantics. Deleting the module, its self-tests and explicit compatibility facade removed 1,492 lines and preserved the complete owner-environment regression.

Therefore `acquire-now`, `defer-acquisition`, fixed `potential_score` weighting, `AcquisitionAssessment`, `ResourceOpportunityBoard` and generic cross-domain ranking are **historical planning treatments, not current World production authority**. The research laws survive; consumer/domain owners form current acquisition/value/priority decisions.

Reopen executable shared Resource planning only when at least two materially different current consumers reproduce the same missing executable responsibility, owner-local resolution fails, and moving the responsibility into World reduces total complexity without importing their value/decision authority.

## Capability Field CF0–CF9 survivors

Canonical compression: [`../../capability-context-doctrine.md`](../../capability-context-doctrine.md), with programme closeout at [`../../capability-field-cf1-cf9-closeout.md`](../../capability-field-cf1-cf9-closeout.md).

The programme establishes that capability is **relational, condition-bound and query-relative**. It retains Actor, Environment and Institution as causal roles when the question needs them while rejecting:

- a persisted `CapabilityField`;
- a universal `Institution` object;
- a complete Environment snapshot;
- a scalar Person/Environment/Institution decomposition;
- a universal coevolution flywheel;
- generic causal state owned by World.

Performance is evidence under tested conditions, not intrinsic actor identity. Production versus expression, assignment versus exposure effect, source versus target transport, and observed association versus identified causal effect remain explicit firewalls. `UNKNOWN` is first-class whenever the stronger causal/capability claim is not identified.
