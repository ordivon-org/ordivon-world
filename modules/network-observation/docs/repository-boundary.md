# Repository Boundary

## Ownership test

A component belongs in Ordivon Link when its primary question is:

> How does an open Task bind one logical communication relationship to mature
> path and identity mechanisms, prove the conditions that actually held, and
> continue when path, endpoint, identity, or participant changes?

## Link candidate responsibilities

- Connectivity Requirement and path/identity observations;
- exact Connectivity Binding to logical source/target and physical mechanisms;
- path-conditioned Artifact and Claim provenance;
- invalidation after path, endpoint, identity, observation, or policy change;
- uncertain delivery/reply reconciliation;
- accountable relationship and participant continuity.

## Mature mechanisms Link reuses

- IP, TCP, UDP, QUIC, HTTP, RPC, queues, and application transports;
- DNS, discovery, load balancers, endpoints, and service identity;
- VPNs, proxies, CNI, service meshes, SDN, namespaces, and firewalls;
- PKI and workload identity;
- telemetry, packet capture, traffic shaping, and fault injection.

Link adapters and experiments may invoke these systems. They must not
reimplement them.

## Foreign ownership

- **Host / semantic Kernel:** Goal, Task, Attempt, Effect, Dispatch, Artifact,
  Claim, verification, participant responsibility, and next action;
- **Edge:** external body/provider placement and execution continuity;
- **Runtime:** trusted-local Workspace, Job, process, and recovery;
- **Security/domain system:** consequence authorization and final validity;
- **network/identity substrate:** routes, endpoints, sockets, packets,
  certificates, service instances, and native lifecycle.

## Current Network World vocabulary

The current Network World contract is valid for the deterministic experiment.
It must not be generalized into a universal production Agent-network ontology,
CNI replacement, or proof of packet-level containment.

A durable Network World abstraction requires two real workloads that fail
without shared Task-conditioned identity, reset, evidence, and invalidation
semantics above mature backends.

## Forbidden dependencies and expansion

- Cloudflare execution, Browser Rendering, R2, or external Fetch implementation;
- Runtime Task, Workspace, Job, process, or Artifact lifecycle;
- a self-developed VPN, proxy, CNI, service mesh, DNS, PKI, transport,
  cryptographic primitive, or generic traffic-control platform;
- automatic host route, DNS, VPN, or firewall mutation before Task-level
  requirement and recovery evidence exists;
- one global identity replacing component-native Task, body, path, service, or
  participant identities.
